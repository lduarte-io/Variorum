# Art direction — the taste record

**Status: observational and propose-only.** Compiled 2026-08-25 from the author's logo
sessions, for the future art-direction sitting (13s's review-and-copy phase is the natural
consumer; 13q where noted). Everything below is a hypothesis extracted from what she
actually picked, passed on, and said — **for her to confirm or overrule at the sitting;
her one word beats any paragraph here.** This is the 13s propose-only posture applied
early: "it's all a taste question."

## The corpus this reads from

- The exploration grid (~40 Recraft generations across five prompted concepts), with her
  picks and passes recorded in-session.
- The **fourteen finalist marks** in this folder (see README) + all editions.
- The **twelve concept-art pieces** in `concept-art/`.
- Her verbatim words along the way, quoted below where they carry weight.

## The evidence — picked vs. passed

**Picked:** the striped V (her top pick — "I think that might be my current top pic
honestly"); the red family broadly ("I like the idea of the red one — even the one that
isn't a V feels very distinct and sharp and interesting"); the calligraphic flourishes;
the filigree and purple chrome Vs ("I really like the V design itself with all the shades
of purple"); then an entire second wave of **engraved/bookplate-tradition ink drawings**
(medallion, bookplate, engraving, fountain book, deco V, echo V); and the whimsical
figure illustrations ("kinda whimsicle concept art that I liked").

**Passed:** the asterisk-star — clever, but dropped because "it's too unclear what the
app actually is" (clarity-of-subject outranks cleverness); the branching-line/"git for
writers" direction (never engaged); knots and abstract monograms; and every AI
"refinement" that thinned or hedged the originals ("all of the refined versions look
worse") — the wispy direction is dead on arrival with her, always.

## The through-lines (hypotheses for ratification)

1. **The world of the printed book, not the world of software.** Every keeper belongs to
   pre-digital print culture: bookplates (ex libris), copper-plate engraving, printers'
   ornaments, deco book-jacket lettering, endpaper patterns, rubrication. Nothing she
   kept reads as tech-brand minimalism, gradient-SaaS, or abstract geometry. The brand's
   home country is the library, not the app store. **TESTED AND CONFIRMED 2026-08-25** —
   the technological direction was deliberately generated and rejected on sight (the
   experiment entry below has her verdict verbatim); this through-line is no longer a
   hypothesis.

2. **The fan of pages is the image she never leaves.** Letterform (striped V, deco V),
   object (the book, fountain book), emblem (the engraving's rosette of open books),
   figure scene (every concept piece) — the fanned page recurs in nearly everything she
   chose. It is also the product's own metaphor: takes fanning from a block. Her eye
   keeps finding the one image where the aesthetic tradition and the product truth
   coincide. **Proposed as the stated brand image**, the thing every asset varies.

3. **Bold structure, rich interior.** She consistently chooses confident, heavy
   silhouettes that carry dense ornament or texture inside. What she rejects is
   timidity — thin, hedged, evenly-gray marks — never ornament. Density is welcome;
   wispiness is not. (A useful brief-sentence for any future commission or generation.)

4. **Ink first; color as a deliberate act.** The foundation is black-and-white. Color
   enters as a single decision: the red ground (`#FC120C` — and red has the earned
   story: rubrication, the scholar's annotation ink), or the purple chrome as a
   deliberate exception living in the icon/hero role. This rhymes with the app's own
   restraint (calm default, theme-token discipline, full-black dark).

5. **Three registers, two of which the app already speaks.**
   - *Editorial deco* (red, flat, sharp — striped V, cards, book, deco V) ≈ the
     **Terminal** voice's sensibility.
   - *Engraved literary* (flourishes, medallion, bookplate, engraving, fountain book)
     ≈ the **Study** voice's sensibility.
   - *Storybook whimsy* (the concept-art figures) — **a register no shipped surface
     owns yet.** 13q's delight thesis ("cute little details…") is its natural in-app
     home; marketing/onboarding/empty states its natural out-of-app home.

6. **People appear only in the illustrations — and always in scale-play with giant
   books** (catching, carrying, riding, heaving them upright). Human warmth enters
   through the storybook register, never through the marks. Proposed as a deliberate
   rule of register separation: marks are objects; illustrations are people-and-objects.

## Tensions and open questions for the sitting

- **Calm surface vs. rich brand.** The shipped app is austere by canon (calm default,
  seamless prose at rest). Proposal: no contradiction — apply "calm default, depth on
  demand" to the brand itself. The app chrome stays quiet; the rich book-world lives at
  the edges: app icon, About, onboarding, empty states, website, export title pages.
  *The app is the writing desk; the brand is the library around it.*
- **Ornament drift.** The engraved/chrome direction can slide toward fantasy-game or
  wine-label if unchecked. The observed guards: strong silhouette first, and every
  ornament grounded in the book world (pages, plates, rosettes — never generic filigree).
- **The red as a token.** `#FC120C` is the generated value, standardized across this
  folder. Whether it becomes a real theme token — and how it behaves in both voices and
  against the full-black dark (R51) — is a 13s tokens question, not settled here.
- **The wordmark is undone.** Recorded lean from the sessions: "variorum" in a bookish
  serif, lowercase or small caps (title-page energy), set in real type beside whichever
  mark wins — never AI-generated lettering.
- **The app icon.** The purple chrome V is concept art for the icon treatment; the
  recorded play is a two-reference render (the chosen mark's real geometry × the chrome
  material) composed for the macOS 26 squircle. The medallion/engraving circles are the
  strongest natively-icon-shaped alternatives.
- **User-selectable icons** (her floated idea, recorded in README): every candidate
  being a V makes a set of alternate icons coherent — one identity, many takes on it,
  which is literally the product's idea. 13q's neighborhood; slotting hers.
- **The pattern tiles.** Three concept pieces tile seamlessly (`girl-and-the-fan`,
  `stepping-off-the-book`, `hanging-from-a-book`) — natural endpapers: website
  backgrounds, onboarding backdrops, loading states, merch.
- **The pixel pet (13q).** If it ships, decide whether pixel art is a deliberate fourth
  register or whether the pet gets drawn in the ink-figure storybook style instead —
  both defensible; flagged only so the sitting decides rather than inherits.
- **The whimsy register, affirmed by her (2026-08-25, on reading this record):** told
  nothing in the app's theming owns the playful side yet, she answered *"maybe there
  should be."* An inclination, not a ruling. If it becomes one, note the two shapes it
  could take: playful *moments* (13q's delight details — the recorded home) vs. a real
  third theme **voice** — the latter touches the R1/R12 hold-at-exactly-two ratification
  and is a canon change only a sitting should make.
- **The technological direction, deliberately re-opened as an experiment (same day):**
  the branching/"git for writers" family died unexplored in the original grid, and on
  reading that observation she got curious ("i didn't think about [it]… i would be
  curious to see what that would look like"). Prompts were provided. This is exactly the
  falsification test this document invites — if she loves the results, through-line 1
  ("the world of the printed book") needs rewriting, not her pick.
  **RESULT (same day): REJECTED, in her words** — "i honestly feel like these just
  aren't really looking that good… these logos just aren't working"; "I don't want my
  logo to look like another tech company's app"; "modern tech logo isn't really as
  appealing to me." A full batch ran (node-graph, commit-graph, pixel, voxel Vs) and
  confirmed through-line 1 instead of falsifying it — the boundary is now evidence, not
  assumption. Two refinements came out of the test: **(a)** "i love the terminal
  aesthetic and tech look" remains true — the terminal is a VOICE the app speaks
  (typography, idioms), never a costume the logo wears; the two coexist by register.
  **(b)** her front-runner language sharpened: the red V "looks new and kind of art
  nouveau"; the cards are "abstract and bold and cool"; the elegant family holds for
  "their elegance." The likely why: her keepers depict the OBJECT (book, page, fan)
  while the tech marks depict the MECHANISM (nodes, branches) — and the mechanism is
  nobody's object of desire.

## What this document is not

Not binding, not a design system, not a decision record. The sitting rules; the author
rules the sitting. When new options arrive, PROCESS.md says how to process them — and
this file should be re-read against them: a new pick that breaks a through-line above
is evidence the hypothesis was wrong, not that the pick is.
