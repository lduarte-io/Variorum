# Short 01 — The Fan (the flagship)

**One idea:** any paragraph can hold many versions, side by side, none of them lost.
**Length:** ~40s. **The gesture:** calm page → fan blooms → swap takes in place →
choose → calm again.

This is the flagship because the fan is the identity — no shipping writing tool
holds parallel versions of *one paragraph* open side by side (everyone else buries
whole-document snapshots in a version-history menu). It is also the single most
demoable thing in the app: the one 15-second gesture communicates the product
better than any explanation, and "calm default, depth on demand" comes across for
free — the viewer literally watches complexity get summoned and dismissed.

---

## Script

**[0–3s — HOOK: the graveyard]**
Screen: a real doc (Google Docs / Word) with old paragraph versions dumped at the
bottom, struck through, half-highlighted.
VO: *"Every writer does this."*
Overlay: **the paragraph graveyard**

**[3–9s — PAIN]**
Slow scroll through the graveyard.
VO: *"You rewrite a paragraph, but you can't bring yourself to delete the old one.
So it goes to the bottom of the doc. Forever."*

**[9–13s — TURN]**
Cut to Variorum. Calm, minimal page — reads as plain beautiful prose.
VO: *"So I built a writing app where you never have to choose."*

**[13–30s — DEMO: the whole sell]**
Fan out a block into 3–4 takes (single-column Takes mode or side-by-side).
VO: *"Any paragraph can hold as many versions as you want — side by side, not
buried in a history menu."*
Click between takes; the paragraph swaps **in place**, the rest of the page holds
perfectly still.
VO: *"Tap one, and the page re-reads itself with that version in."*
Choose one; the fan folds away; the page is calm again.
VO: *"Pick your favorite. The rest stay open as long as you want them."*

**[30–40s — KICKER]**
Calm page. Title card with the striped V.
VO: *"A 'variorum' is an edition of a text that preserves its variant readings.
Now it's an app."*
Overlay: **Variorum — I'm building it in public, follow along**

---

## Notes

- The etymology is the closing line on purpose: the word's dictionary definition
  IS the pitch, and it makes the name feel inevitable rather than obscure.
- The demo takes must read as **genuinely different drafts** (one lyrical, one
  terse, one restructured) so a viewer gets the idea in a single glance — see
  `filming-notes.md` § Staging.
- First frame = the graveyard doc, never a logo.
