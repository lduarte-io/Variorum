# Short 03 — Delete Without Fear

**One idea:** nothing you write is ever gone — delete, split, merge, rewrite are
all safe, and the app quietly insures the moments you'd regret.
**Length:** ~40–45s. **The gesture:** delete a paragraph → the tray → Put back;
pin a savepoint → wreck the text → restore it.

Where Short 01 sells keeping versions *open*, this one sells the deeper religion
underneath: mutation never reaches anything you decided to keep. It speaks to
the hoarder in every writer — the strikethroughs, the "maybe later" files — and
it is the trust argument: you can be bold in this app because nothing is
irreversible.

---

## Script

**[0–3s — HOOK]**
Screen: a paragraph selected, the cursor hovering over delete. A beat of
hesitation.
VO: *"Deleting a paragraph is the scariest thing a writer does."*
Overlay: **just delete it (you won't)**

**[3–10s — PAIN]**
Quick cuts: strikethrough text, a comment saying "keep??", a file called
`maybe-later-FINAL.docx`.
VO: *"So we don't. We strikethrough. We hoard. We paste it into a 'maybe later'
doc we never open again."*

**[10–15s — TURN]**
Cut to Variorum, calm page.
VO: *"I'm building a writing app where delete is actually safe."*

**[15–35s — DEMO, three beats]**

*Beat A — the tray.* Delete a whole paragraph — gone from the page. Open the
note's tray: there it is. Click **Put back** — it returns to its exact place.
VO: *"Delete a paragraph, and it waits in the note's tray. One click puts it
back — right where it was."*

*Beat B — savepoints.* Hit the savepoint shortcut on a paragraph you like. Then
rewrite it — badly, visibly worse. Open its history, restore the savepoint: the
good version is back.
VO: *"Any version you love, you can pin as a savepoint — rewrite fearlessly,
come back whenever."*

*Beat C — the quiet insurance.* Calm page, nothing clicked.
VO: *"And at the moments you'd regret most — closing a note mid-edit, a big
structural change — it quietly saves for you. You don't have to remember to be
careful."*

**[35–45s — KICKER]**
VO: *"Nothing you write is ever more than a click from coming back. That's the
whole religion. Variorum."*
Overlay: **a variorum preserves the variants — follow along**

---

## Notes

- Beat A is the money shot: the paragraph must visibly return to its **exact
  slot** in one click — rehearse so the camera holds the same scroll position
  through delete → tray → put back.
- Beat B's bad rewrite should be comically worse (viewers should wince), so the
  restore lands as relief.
- Beat C is deliberately undemonstrated — a claim over a calm page. Showing an
  auto-savepoint firing would mean showing a settings pane; trust the VO.
- Do NOT try to also show structural undo/redo, whole-note Versions, or merge
  reversibility here — same religion, separate videos. One gesture per short.
