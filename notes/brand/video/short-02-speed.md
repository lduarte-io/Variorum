# Short 02 — The Speed Stunt

**One idea:** fast at any size, by architectural law — the Apple Notes failure
mode, designed out.
**Length:** ~40s. **The gesture:** the same huge document in Apple Notes and in
Variorum, side by side; one chokes, one glides.

Stunt-comparison videos are a native genre on shorts platforms, and the pain is
one of the most widely felt among writers: everyone with a long note has watched
Apple Notes start to drag. This short positions speed not as an optimization but
as a *law of the app* — which is the true story (P1: nothing may cost more than
what's on screen).

---

## Script

**[0–4s — HOOK: the setup]**
Split screen, vertical: the SAME long document open in Apple Notes (top) and
Variorum (bottom). A visible word count.
VO: *"Same document. Fifty thousand words. Two apps."*
Overlay: **50,000 words**

**[4–11s — PAIN]**
Scroll Notes — it stutters. Click into the middle and type — keystrokes lag
behind the fingers.
VO: *"Apple Notes starts choking past ten thousand words. Every keystroke makes
the whole document do work."*

**[11–16s — TURN]**
VO: *"The app I'm building has a law: nothing is allowed to cost more than
what's on screen."*
Overlay: **cost ∝ what's on screen — never the document**

**[16–32s — DEMO]**
In Variorum, same doc: fling-scroll — instant. Jump to the end — instant. Click
into the middle of the novel and type — letters land under the cursor with zero
lag.
VO: *"Scroll it. Jump to the end. Type in the middle."*
Then the flex: open a fan on a block mid-novel — full machinery, full speed.
VO: *"Even the heavy machinery — ten versions of one paragraph — runs at full
speed in the middle of a book."*

**[32–40s — KICKER]**
VO: *"Your novel should never lag. Variorum."*
Overlay: **Variorum — building it in public, follow along**

---

## Notes — HONESTY RULES (this one especially)

- **Film real behavior only.** No time-remapping either side: never speed up our
  footage or slow down theirs. If a claim needs editing tricks, the claim is
  wrong.
- Pick the document size where the difference is genuinely visible on camera on
  the day of filming — if Notes behaves at 50k on this machine, grow the doc
  until the (real) failure mode shows, and put the true number in the overlay.
- Record at 60fps — scrolling smoothness IS the content of this video; 30fps
  motion blur would hide the very difference being demonstrated.
- Same content in both apps, pasted moments apart, both visible in one frame at
  the moment of the stunt — the honesty must be legible, not just true.
- The typing beat lands harder than the scrolling beat (input latency is felt in
  the viewer's own fingers); give it the closeup.
