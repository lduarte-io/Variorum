# Variorum — brand mark finalists

Fourteen production-clean vector marks. **THE LOGO IS CHOSEN: the striped V — ratified by
the author 2026-08-25 ("yeah, that's going to be the logo").** The rest of the family
stands as supporting cast (secondary marks, ornament, icon concepts), per TASTE.md.
The choosing surface is **`variorum-family-sheet.svg`** (per mark: light ground over dark
ground; `.png` twins of the sheets are pre-rendered copies for apps that balk at SVG).
The pipeline that produced everything here is documented in **`PROCESS.md`** — new options
the author drops in later get the same treatment; `sources/` archives the raw generations
(C2PA metadata stripped) and `tools/` the scripts as they ran. **`TASTE.md`** is the
art-direction taste record compiled from her picks and words across the sessions —
propose-only, written for the future design sitting.

## Provenance

Generated by the author with Recraft (true-SVG output) at the logo sessions of
2026-08-24/25, from prompts built on the app's own metaphor — the fan of takes.
Each file was then cleaned in-session: full-canvas background rects and AI artifact
specks removed, viewBoxes cropped to the mark +10% margin, and painted-on details
converted to real geometry where possible. Nothing was redrawn; the geometry is
exactly what she picked.

## Naming scheme

- `-master` — one-color ink (`#111111`) on **transparent**; the workhorse asset
  (favicon, menu-bar template glyph, watermark, single-color print). Only the
  one-color marks have one.
- `-faithful` / `-faithful-red` — exactly as generated (cleaned), on its own ground.
- `-white` / `-on-white`, `-on-cream`, `-on-red` — light editions.
- `-dark` / `-white-on-black` — dark-mode editions (full inversion for the two-tone marks).
- `variorum-v-final-sheet.svg` — the striped V's colorway sheet incl. the 64/32/16 px test.

## Constants

- **Red `#FC120C`** — the actual generated red, standardized across all red editions
  (candidate brand accent; rubrication precedent — red is the scholar's annotation ink).
- **Ink `#111111`**, **cream `#F3EBD6` / `#F8F6E5`** (per-mark, as generated).

## Practicalities (flags, not legal advice — noted 2026-08-25; her findings same day)

*[removed for publication]*
- **Copyright vs. trademark:** purely AI-generated art carries limited copyright
  protection under current US guidance — but a LOGO's real shield is **trademark**
  (use in commerce + registration), which does not depend on authorship. Setting the
  wordmark in real type adds a human-authored layer besides.
