# The MCP invisibility residuals — the one-place ledger

**Her framing (2026-11-05, the VX-809 morning round):** *"invisible where it would be impossible to hide that **something** exists."* This file is the complete list of those impossibilities — kept in one place at her ask so marketing, the privacy policy and the public MCP page can be aligned against exactly what is promised. The law itself (R-825: every MCP answer is a pure function of the client's reachable state) and its pin (the two-store catalog, 209 arms) are MCPSpec's; this ledger enumerates the recorded exceptions and residuals, each with the exact words the wire speaks.

## What NEVER crosses, residuals included

A connected AI never receives — in any answer, refusal, listing, cursor or log — a hidden thing's **name, title, kind, count, content, tags, dates, or identity**. A private or ungranted thing answers byte-identically to a thing that does not exist (the fence's not-shared shape). The residuals below leak at most ONE bit each: *that something unreachable exists*, in corners where reality makes even that impossible to hide.

## The forced-bit refusal class (a refusal where a hidden-less store would succeed)

These acts cannot lie-and-succeed (ownership, capacity and seals are real), so the refusal itself is the bit.

1. **Capacity (QUESTIONS #218, kept at A-809-4 S-1).** Adding a note to a granted book that hidden (Private) members have filled refuses with her capacity words. The AI learns the book is full — never what fills it. *(On a granted book, unreadable members are by definition Private-marked ones, so this is the residual that brushes "hides its existence" most directly; her #218 word accepted it.)*
2. **Ownership (P-809-2).** `add_note_to_book` on a note an UNREADABLE book claims refuses: *"That note can't be added to this book — something beyond your reach holds it. The author can add it in Variorum."* The owned-vs-membered kind collapses; nothing else crosses. (A READABLE claimant gets her doors' full words instead — those describe reachable state and are not a residual.)
3. **The frozen holder (A-809-4 S-2).** `move_note_to_wing` on a note an UNREADABLE finished or sealed book holds refuses in the move's verb — never "finished" or "sealed" (those are the hidden book's facts).
4. **The book move's two arms (P-809-10 / A-809-15).** `move_book_to_wing` refused by an unreadable frozen stay-behind holder, or because the move would shed a member's privacy fence: *"That book can't be moved — something beyond your reach holds one of its notes. The author can move it in Variorum."*

## The standing residuals outside the refusal class

5. **The tag vocabulary (P-809-9, kept 2026-11-05).** A wing's tag paths are wing-level configuration: answers truthfully say a path was *adopted* even when its only wearer is hidden. One bit — "this word exists as a tag here" — never which note or how many. (The alternative, reporting "made" for an adopted tag, was rejected as a false word on the wire.)
6. **The book cursor (VX-859 — OPEN, closing).** `list_notes`' book-page cursor resumes by a raw position key whose bytes are insertion-history-dependent. Pre-ruled 2026-11-05: the id-resuming cursor builds with VX-808, retiring this entry when it lands.

## The scope words (how partiality is said without counting)

Landed answers that list what an act touched carry fixed, always-present sentences — byte-identical whether hidden things exist or not: *"Books beyond your reach, if any, are not listed."* and *"Notes beyond your reach, if any, are not counted."* These are honesty-by-humility, not leaks: a constant sentence can't hint.

## A ready-to-paste public paragraph (hers to place — privacy policy / the public MCP page)

> Anything you haven't shared is invisible to a connected AI — it can't be read, listed, named, or counted, and a private thing answers exactly as if it didn't exist. There is one honest exception class: where reality makes it impossible to hide that *something* exists, Variorum says that one fact and nothing more. For example, adding a note to a book that unseen notes have filled is refused — the AI learns the book is full, never what fills it; and a note an unshared book holds can't be taken from it — the AI learns "something beyond your reach holds it," never what. In every such case the refusal names nothing, counts nothing, and describes nothing.
