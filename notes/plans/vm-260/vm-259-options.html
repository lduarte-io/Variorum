VM-259 — the Library's top gives way (three options)

Her words: "the top row should also collapse while I'm scrolling — I'm open to ideas on how that should happen… the app just feels like it's fighting for space."

All three start from the same top at batch 2's corrected sizes (the wing line's 17-pt glyphs, the 36-in-44 search box at corner 8, the 13-pt near-square filter chips). Each phone really scrolls — scroll the list down, then nudge up. The paging law (VM-68) is untouched by all three; what differs is only what the chrome does.

A — the whole top slips away

Scrolling down, the whole top (wing line, search, filters) rides off with the paper; any upward nudge brings it all back at once. Nothing floats while reading — the most room, zero chrome. Bookcase · Starred: their top lines slip the same way. Cost: New Note and the Trash need one nudge up first.
