# 13n-a sample book — the PT/EN live-round fixture

`o ladrão de raios/` is a FOLDER of Variorum documents (the Level 0 format,
R-13n-8): two chapter folders and one top-level file. Import it with
**File ▸ Import…** (pick the folder) or drop the folder on the Bookcase: it
lands as ONE book — "o ladrão de raios", chapters "1 o semideus" and
"2 a excursão" (digit-aware order), four notes (the top-level `posfácio`
seated unfiled, after the chapters), every Portuguese paragraph the chosen
take with its English take labeled "english" beside it, and one unlabeled
take with internal line breaks (`01 abertura.md`, third block). One ⌥⌘Z
removes the whole book; ⇧⌥⌘Z brings it back.

The prose is ORIGINAL sample text written for this fixture (two opening
lines echo the sitting mockups' §8 sample); it is not the novel's text.
