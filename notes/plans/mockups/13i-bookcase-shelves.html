Three directions below; A is the recommendation and is live (click shelf names; click the masthead numbers to collapse them; click “details” to hide the right meta; collapse shelf headers in All).

THE CENSUS LINE (this revision — her idea made structural): the counts leave the masthead and become their own quiet second line, where each term is a FILTER — click “3 gatherings” to solo gatherings across the current view, click again to clear (selection = ink + weight, the same idiom; in the build, a shelf with no matches folds away). The ⋯ opens the full census as a dropdown with counts — owned · living · sealed — for the longer tail, so the line itself stays three terms. The ruled collapse gesture now applies to THIS line (fold to ··, remembered). At its right end, order · shelf order ▾ — the view-local sort: shelf order (default) · last edited. It overlays, never rewrites your arrangement — A31's view-local precedent, one level up.

Direction A's claims: the shelf line is read-quiet — muted words, the active one in ink and weight, generous space doing the separating. Counts live on HOVER (and in the overflow panel), “default” lives in the right-click, glyphs are opt-in per shelf and monochrome. The details word at the line's end is your ruled setting: it hides every right-hand number (counts, words, status words) — remembered per surface — while arrivals stay visible even with details off, because arrivals ask for judgment and numbers don't. The masthead numbers collapse to ·· on click (your ruled gesture), remembered. Past ~ten shelves a trailing muted “⋯ 6 more” word opens the type-to-filter panel, unchanged from v1. Drag a book onto a shelf name to re-shelve (hairline outline on drag-over only); drag names to reorder; All stacks with the Library's own gray section labels, collapsible.
