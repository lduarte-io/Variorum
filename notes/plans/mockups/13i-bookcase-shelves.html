What State A claims: the Bookcase opens solo on your default shelf (right-click any shelf name: Rename · Set Glyph · Make Default · Delete — deleting a shelf returns its books to the first shelf, never touches a book; “default” shows beside the count on hover). All stacks every shelf with collapsible headers — a real bookcase read top to bottom. Shelf names are also drop targets: drag a book row onto one to re-shelve it (a hairline square outline appears around the name while dragging over — the only container the strip ever draws). Drag the names themselves, or the rows in the overflow panel, to reorder shelves. “2 arrivals” on Encryption Explained is the inbox surfacing on the bookcase — accent-colored because it asks for judgment; everything else stays muted. The right-meta word carries the vocabulary: gathering · living · owned · finished · sealed.
The strip’s look (this revision): no pills, no fills, no borders — shelf names are set exactly like a mode row: quiet sans, generous spacing, the active one accented with a thin rule beneath, counts whispered on hover only. The optional glyph is one grapheme, monochrome — the callout frame’s ruled precedent — drawn from a curated case of printer’s ornaments (❧ ❦ ☙ ✶ ✦ ❖ † ◆) with free Unicode entry for anything else (a black heart, a dot, a hieroglyph). No emoji, no color: the glyph inherits the text’s ink, so the strip stays typographic — ornament, not decoration.

Why not browser-style tabs as the primary? Tabs truncate past ~8, hide every shelf you are not on, and have no “All” that reads like a bookcase. The chip strip IS the tab idea, but it wraps instead of truncating, carries counts, takes drops, and keeps the stacked All view one click away.
Why not a dropdown as the primary? A dropdown hides the whole shelf vocabulary behind a click — with one shelf you would never learn shelves exist, which fails your “the verb stays visible” ruling. It survives as the overflow panel, where search earns its place.
Free riders: ⌘K already gives “go to shelf” for nothing; the sidebar item renames Shelf → Bookcase in the same vocabulary sweep as wings; pinned books keep floating to the top of their own shelf.
