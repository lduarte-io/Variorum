### F1 · MAJOR · `App/VariorumMobile/.../MobileFirstWriteFurniture.swift:156-158, 240` + `SampleLibraryFreshStore.swift:95,134` — a fresh phone install that joins an existing account seeds the Prince INTO that account's library

**What's wrong.** The phone's fresh-store fact is taken once at launch (`prime()` → `storeWasFreshAtLaunch`) and spent at her first write (`freshness: .takenAtLaunch(fact ?? false)`; `mayMint` answers `resuming || fresh` — the store is never re-read). **Why it's spec drift, not an accepted residual.** R-633-16's phone arm (plan, "Her D1/D3 rulings"): "its first-write occasion seeds the launch book on a fresh store only — an existing phone store never gains the Prince by update." At seed time this store holds her entire library — by the shared predicate's own definition (`storeIsFresh`: "never held writing") it is not fresh. The Mac deliberately judges at the deciding reads AFTER the sync gate for exactly this flow ("a new device joining her library finds writing and declines" — `SampleLibraryFreshStore.swift:50-54`, pinned by `anAccountStoreIsJudgedByWhatItsFirstRoundBrought`); the phone has no equivalent. The U2b record's residuals record the Mac's ≤20 s persisted-session window and the signed-out-install-then-join case (R-633-18's accepted class, where the seed happens while the store is genuinely fresh and then travels) — but NOT this window, where the library arrives first and the seed writes into it. It is the one place the two platforms' judgments genuinely diverge, on what is arguably the most common new-phone flow (install → sign in → write, one session). Relaunching the app between sign-in and first write closes the window (prime re-reads), which is why it's MAJOR and not BLOCKER.

**Red-on-old pin** (`MobileFirstWriteFurnitureTests`): prime the furniture on an empty store (fact: fresh); then land "her library" in the store (a book + a note via ops, standing in for the pull — exactly `anEarlierBuildsStore`'s shape); then fire the occasion. Expect `.existingStore` and no Prince row ever. On the current tree the occasion answers `.seeded` and the Prince is in the library — red.

**Fix.** The honest options reach product behavior, so the SHAPE is hers (a `notes/QUESTIONS.md` entry at the merge), but the review's recommendation: **re-check at spend time when the session has since joined an account** — in `run()`, when `gate.belongsToAnAccount()` is true (sync configured / an account present), do not spend the launch fact: hand `.readAtDecision`-class behavior or decline outright (`.takenAtLaunch(false)`), recorded. A genuinely fresh signed-out phone (the common fresh case) keeps today's road untouched; a phone that joined an account between launch and first write declines — matching the Mac's account rule. Cost to name for her: a phone-FIRST user who signs into a brand-new empty account before their first write never gets the Prince, and the phone has no restore door (the U2b MOBILE PARITY line already records that gap). Alternative shapes: invalidate the fact when a pull has applied rows since prime; or accept and RECORD the window as a residual beside R-633-18's (her "it's kinda whatever" was said about the signed-out-install join case, not this one — it should at least be put in front of her).
