# VM-44 — grade 1: the shell, the Library and books (everything outside a note)

Draft working record, not canon. Grader 1 of two; grader 2 has everything inside a note.

**What was graded.** All 69 screenshots in `atlas/screens/` that show a surface outside a note (listed per row in the table, so coverage can be
checked), the two evidence crops that concern the shell (`atlas/findings/W3-…`, `W18-…`), and the frames of `rec-01` and `rec-02` as sampled in
`W18-…`. Primary pass only: Study voice, light, Classic colours, iPhone 17 Pro (402 × 874 pt). The missing sea-glass palette is a known gap
(inventory F1) and is not marked down per screen. Numbers are cited from `audit/G-visual-metrics.md` (G§, T-ids) and `atlas/walk-findings.md`
(W-ids); where a number is "measured from the capture" it is my own reading of the PNG.

**How to read a grade.** A = right for a phone as it stands · B = sound, small repairs · C = usable but visibly off · D = gets in her way ·
F = fails the screen's job. The seven axes are the brief's: legibility, touch, clarity, calm, craft, native fit, flow.

**The five worst problems, in order.**
1. **She cannot tell notes apart or find them.** Library and Journal rows are a raw date with no preview (words 4, 5), and search reads titles only (W6),
   so a note written on the phone — titled by its day — cannot be found by anything in it.
2. **No wings, no shelves.** Every list is all nine wings at once: 23 books in one run, "Ideas inbox" twice, `#draft` and `#idea` twice in the tag
   manager (words 4; W5, W11).
3. **The chrome is 11.5 pt everywhere and cannot grow** — including the text she types into the capture box and the search field (G§1.3), and it
   stays 11.5 pt at the largest system text size (W19) (words 1, 7).
4. **Touch.** Navigation, settings and destructive acts are text links about 14 pt tall: the tab bar, "+ New Note", "Settings", the trash can,
   every Settings row, "Put back" / "Delete Now", "Done" on every sheet (G§2: 103 of 107 control sites under 44 pt) (words 2). Several acts exist only
   behind a long press with nothing on screen to say so.
5. **The capture box.** At rest a 34 pt dashed outline the list runs under; focused, a half-screen dashed rectangle with the tab bar wedged between it
   and the keyboard; Return opens the note, drops words typed during the hand-off (W1) and freezes mid-push for about 0.7 s (W18) (words 3, 6).

## 3. Per screen — the three most damaging problems

### 1 · The twelve-words card (`00-first-launch`, `00-words-card-redacted`)
1. **It is the first thing a fresh phone shows** — before the app has a name, a shelf or a note. The Mac's rule is that nothing greets a first open;
   the phone opens on its highest-stakes screen. A Mac user adding the phone is asked to write down words for a library the join is about to replace
   (D-06, D-39).
2. **"Variorum won't show them again" with one door.** No copy, no "later", no explanation of what a passphrase would add beyond an 11.5 pt
   two-line caption in the muted ink.
3. **Centred 16 pt serif in the muted ink (3.4:1)** for the paragraph that matters most; the caption under the words is 11.5 pt. (Words 1.)

Reason for the B grades: the layout is tidy, the title is 21 pt serif and the one button is a real button about 41 pt tall.

### 2–4 · Empty shelf, Library and Starred (`01-empty-shelf`, `01-empty-library`, `01-empty-starred`)
1. **The road in for someone who already uses Variorum is a footnote:** "Have an account? Settings ▸ Sync signs you in." in the faint ink at 11.5 pt,
   pointing at a 14 pt-tall "Settings" word (T4). This is the launch's central road.
2. **The empty shelf has three competing starts** — "+ New Book", "today's entry" and the capture box about 500 pt further down — and the empty Library
   still draws five filters, a sort glyph and an "Archived" link over nothing.
3. **Empty Starred teaches the app's hidden grammar by necessity:** "press and hold a note or a book, then choose Star" — the only way to star
   anything is an invisible long press. (Words 2.)

### 5 · The capture box on an empty store, and the first write (`01-empty-quick-entry-focused`, `01-first-write-typed`)
1. **A half-screen dashed rectangle with one line of 11.5 pt text in its corner**, on a screen with nothing else on it. (Words 3, 1.)
2. **The tab bar sits between the box and the keyboard** (G§3.3), so the first thing under her words is navigation, not the keys.
3. **"⏎ opens it" is the box's whole instruction**, in the faint ink; "✓ saved" and "open ↵" are 11.5 pt words 14 pt tall (T11).

### 6 · After the first write (`01-shelf-after-first-write`, `01-library-after-first-write`, `01-starred-after-first-write`)
1. **Her first note is unrecognisable in the Library:** it is the row "2026-11-12", third in a list that is otherwise 28 chapters of The Prince. (Words 5.)
2. **The sample book floods the Library** — every chapter of The Prince is a Library row, so the first real screen of notes is someone else's book.
3. **The furniture's names are lowercase in Study** ("journal", "the prince", chapter labels "about this text") beside her own capitalised books —
   a Terminal idiom stored as data, which reads as a typo in the voice she has named as the direction.

### 7 · Home: the shelf (`10-shelf-rest`, `10-shelf-books-scrolled`, `24-shelf-after-quick-note-filed`)
1. **Everything in one bunch.** 23 books from nine wings in one run with no shelves; two rows read "Ideas inbox" with nothing to tell them apart (W5).
   The header says "Shelf · 23 books" in 11.5 pt and that is the screen's only title. (Words 4.)
2. **The list runs under the capture box:** the last visible row ("Manuscript · 1 note · #draft") is sliced by the dashed border with no gap or fade (W2),
   and "+ New Book" is the 24th row, hidden under the box until the inner list is scrolled to its end (W3). (Words 3.)
3. **The header's doors are tiny:** the trash can is 14 × 14 pt, "+ New Note" and "Settings" are words 14 pt tall (T2–T4); the Journal's "today"
   chip is 51 × 20 pt (T6). Each row's meta is right-aligned 11.5 pt in the muted ink at 3.4:1. (Words 2, 1.)

Also: the room stops at the safe areas — the strip behind the status bar and the strip under the tab bar are pure white (`#FFFFFF`) against the
cream room (`#FAF8F3`), measured from the capture (W4).

### 8 · The tab bar (every root)
1. **Three 11.5 pt words whose tap areas are 35–50 × 14 pt** (T1). The inactive two are in the faint ink at about 2.1:1. (Words 2, 1.)
2. **It rides above the keyboard** whenever the capture box or the search field is focused, spending about 35 pt between her text and the keys.
3. **It is gone on every pushed screen**, so moving from a book to the Library means backing out first (W-007). It says "Shelf" where the Mac says "Bookcase".

### 9–10 · The capture box at rest, focused and typed (`20-…`, `21-…`, `22-…` ×2, `findings/W18-…`)
1. **Return opens the note, and words typed during the hand-off are lost** — the rest land at the start of the note (W1). The same hand-off freezes
   mid-push for about 0.7 s and then raises a second keyboard (W18, `rec-02`). (Words 6.) The box can hold one paragraph only.
2. **Focused, its top border is drawn straight through the "Running log" row's text** (`21-…`), and the area is a fixed half of what the keyboard leaves
   however little is typed: two lines of 11.5 pt text in a dashed rectangle about 170 pt tall (`22-…-two-lines`). (Words 3.)
3. **At rest it is a 34 pt outline** (measured; G§3.2) in a dash at about 1.9–2.1:1 with an 11.5 pt hint — lower and fainter than the rows around it,
   yet the only bordered object on the screen. Typed text is 82 % of the Mac's own capture size (G§3.5). (Words 1, 7.)

### 11 · Shelf long-press menus (`11-shelf-book-longpress-menu`, `11-shelf-journal-longpress-menu`)
1. **The menu is the only road** to star, pin or delete a book, and nothing on the row says it exists.
2. **"Unpin from Shelf Top" sits beside "Unstar"** — the Mac retired Pin into the star (W22).
3. **No rename, details, move to shelf or move to wing** from the row.

### 12 · New Book sheet (`12-create-book-sheet`)
1. **The three kinds ("Book · Journal Book · Smart Book") are 11.5 pt words 14 pt tall** (T33), and "Cancel" is the same (T31).
2. **The name field is not focused on arrival**, and "Create Book" is pinned about 650 pt below it with an empty sheet between.
3. **It can only mint in Home, unshelved** — there is nowhere on the sheet to say which wing or shelf (W-072).

### 13 · Starred (`14-starred-page`)
1. **Books and notes are told apart only by a faint 11.5 pt word at the far right** ("book", "note").
2. **No wing, no preview, no date** — "Full draft — v3" is a note in which book, in which wing?
3. **Unstar is long-press only.**

### 14 · System confirms and prompts (six files)
Nothing damaging. Two small things: the title "Empty Trash…" keeps the door's ellipsis, and the New Chapter prompt's "Create" is greyed with no
hint why until a name is typed. The copy is the app's best: "“Essays” goes to the Trash for 30 days. Its notes stay in the library."

### 15 · The shelf at the largest text size (`96-shelf-xxxl-text`)
1. **Only the book names grow**; the header, the meta, the today chip, the box's hint and the tab bar stay 11.5 pt (W19). (Words 1.)
2. **The grown names truncate** ("The Lighthouse K…") against meta that did not move.
3. **So the system's text-size setting cannot rescue her** — the one lever a phone gives a reader does nothing to the chrome.

### 16 · The Library, All (`30-library-rest-all`, `30-library-scrolled-1`, `-2`, `14-after-band-swipe-left`)
1. **Rows that say nothing:** of the first eight rows, five read only "2026-11-12". Further down a dated note prints its date twice
   ("2026-11-11 … 2026-11-11"). No preview, no first words, no book, no wing. (Words 5, 4.)
2. **One bunch:** about 150 notes and 23 books from nine wings interleaved in one scroll, with raw ISO dates and no day or month headers. (Words 4.)
3. **The controls are the smallest things on the screen:** five filter words 14 pt tall that may shrink to 8 pt (T15), a 13 × 13 pt sort glyph (T16),
   a 14 × 14 pt trash can; the list slides under them with a hard cut (W23). (Words 2.)

### 17 · Filters: Books, Notes, Bookless (`31-library-filter-*`)
1. **Bookless opens on four rows of "2026-11-12"** — the Pool is where quick notes land, and it is the least legible list in the app. (Words 5.)
2. **Books repeats the shelf with a different row** (regular weight, no long-press menu, G§4.4) and the same twin "Ideas inbox".
3. **"Bookless" and "Scraps" are not the Mac's words** (Pool, Snippets), so the two devices name the same rooms differently.

### 18 · Scraps (`31-library-filter-scraps`)
1. **Rows do nothing** — a scrap cannot be opened, edited, moved or deleted here (G§4.4).
2. **"+ new scrap" is an 11.5 pt link** the walk's driver could not even find as a button.
3. **The group picker is not visible in the capture**, so which pool these four belong to is unsaid.

### 19 · Sort menu (`32-library-sort-menu`)
1. **Its opener is a 13 × 13 pt glyph** (T16). (Words 2.)
2. **"date · created · edited · last meaningful edit"** — lowercase in Study, "date" is ambiguous, and there is no direction.
3. Nothing else; the menu itself is the system's and is right.

### 20 · Search (`33-library-search-*`)
1. **Titles only.** "ferry" returns "Nothing found." though at least four notes contain it; "lighthouse" misses the notes that have it in their text (W6).
   With quick notes titled by date, nothing she writes on the phone can be found by its words. (Words 4.)
2. **"Nothing found." says nothing about why** — no hint that bodies are not searched, no scope, no wing.
3. **The field is 11.5 pt and has no Cancel or clear**; it is about 39 pt tall at rest and about 32 pt once focused (measured from `30-…` against
   `33-…-focused`), so the filters jump as the keyboard rises, and the tab bar rides above the keys. (Words 1.)

### 21 · Note long-press menu (`34-library-note-longpress-menu`)
1. **Star and Delete are the whole menu** — no move to a book, tag, duplicate, archive or move to wing.
2. **It is the only door to either act** from the list.
3. Nothing else.

### 22 · The Archive room (`35-archive-room`)
1. **"Unarchive" and "Open note" are 11.5 pt links 14 pt tall** (T98). (Words 2.)
2. **"kept — no clock"** is the room's only explanation, in the faint ink; the facets are "All · Notes" because archived books have no home on the
   phone at all (W20).
3. **Its door is the last row of the Library** — under All, the last of about 150 rows — and the room has two headers: an empty system bar with a
   back button, then its own 11.5 pt "Archived" label.

### 23 · The Trash (`36-trash-room`, `36-trash-room-scrolled`, `13-trash-from-shelf`)
1. **"Put back" and "Delete Now" are 14 pt-tall words 18 pt apart** on every row (T97), and "Empty Trash…" is a 14 pt-tall word in the header
   beside the sort word (T95). The restoring act and the destroying act are the same size, side by side. (Words 2.)
2. **Six filter words in a row** ("All · Notes · Books · Blocks · Snippet groups · Originals"), two of them jargon, at 11.5 pt.
3. **Alarm-red links run down the whole screen**, one per row, so the calmest room in the app reads as a warning.

What is right here: each row says what the thing is, where it came from and how many days are left.

### 24 · Book contents (eight files)
1. **Read-and-open only.** No row has a long press, a swipe or a reorder: a note cannot be moved, pinned, removed or put in a chapter (inventory F9).
   "+ New Note" and "+ New Chapter" are 11.5 pt links after the last row. (Words 9.)
2. **The list hides what the Mac shows:** no pin mark on the pinned note (`48-…-pinned`), no sign of three waiting arrivals (`46-…`, W15), a count of 8
   over a list of 7 (W7), a gathering that looks exactly like a book (`45-…`), and a finished book still offering "+ New Note" (`47-…`, W14).
3. **No preview and no stamp on any row**, and chapter labels are 11.5 pt in the muted ink — in The Prince they are lowercase and repeat the row beneath
   ("about this text" / "about this text"). Above it all sits an empty system bar, then the title: two headers for one screen. (Words 5, 1.)

### 25 · The daily Journal (`44-book-daily-journal`)
1. **Every row is the same raw date twice** — "2026-11-11 … 2026-11-11" — and nothing about the entry. (Words 5.)
2. **There is no Today door here**; "+ New Note" is below the twelfth row, and "+ New Chapter" is offered on a journal.
3. **The first row is "Your journal"**, the furniture's own note, sitting undated above the entries.

### 26 · Book Details (`41-book-details-sheet`, `48-book-details-lighthouse`)

2. **"note 1 of 7" is 11 pt in the faint ink**, and the count disagrees with the shelf's 8 (W7).

## 4. Cross-cutting problems (the design-language sheet's agenda)

1. **No phone type scale.** The phone wears the Mac's tokens unchanged; 241 of 352 text sites are the Mac's 11.5 pt sidebar size (G§0). The pattern on
   every screen is one 16 pt serif thing surrounded by 11.5 pt everything-else. Needed: a phone layer (title, row title, body, meta, control) with a
   13 pt floor, and Dynamic Type through it.
2. **Words standing in for buttons.** Navigation, sheet "Done"s, row acts and destructive acts are plain text at caption size with no shape and a
   glyph-sized hit area. Needed: a small set of real controls — a 44 pt round button, a capsule, a full-width row — used everywhere.
3. **Contrast.** The muted and faint inks are 3.4:1 and 2.1:1 on the Study room (G§4.2) and carry nearly all meta, hints and inactive states. On the
   sea-glass room the muted ink is 3.1:1. Small text makes this worse.
4. **Long-press-only acts.** Star, pin, delete, unstar and every tag act have no visible door. A phone list wants swipe actions and a visible "more".
5. **Wing-blindness.** Shelf, Library, Starred, the tag manager and the pickers list every wing at once; nothing on any screen names a wing.
6. **One object, several rows.** A note row is built four ways (Library, book, Archive, Trash) and a book row two ways (G§4.4). Dates are raw
   `2026-11-12` keys everywhere a stamp appears.
7. **Headers.** Roots have an 11.5 pt "path line" where a title should be; pushed screens have an empty system bar and then their own masthead; every
   sheet hand-rolls "Title … Done". There is no large title anywhere outside the note and Read.
8. **Edges.** The room stops at the safe areas (white strips top and bottom, W4); lists slide under pinned headers and under the capture box with a
   hard cut (W2, W23); the tab bar rides the keyboard.
9. **Spacing has no scale** — 19 padding values and 7 radii (G§4.1). Rows land near 42–44 pt by accident of padding; nothing declares a row height.
10. **No lists, forms or toggles.** Everything is a hand-built stack, which is why Settings cannot scroll and nothing answers the system text size.
11. **Vocabulary drift from the Mac.** Shelf / Bookcase, Bookless / Pool, Scraps / Snippets, "Pin", "Smart Book", "Lock Note" — and lowercase stored
    names from the first-write furniture.
12. **No receipts.** Filing a quick note, starring and deleting say nothing; the walk found no status line firing on the shell at all.
13. **Counts and marks disagree or are missing** — 8 against 7, no pin mark, no arrivals, a gathering indistinguishable from a book.

## 5. What is good and must survive

- **The Study voice itself.** Iowan Old Style names on the warm room read like a table of contents; the lists are quiet, with no boxes, icons or
  colour for its own sake. Her word is that the look is fine — the grading agrees. Resize it; do not restyle it.
- **Book Read** (`42-…`). A real page at a comfortable measure. It is the standard for body text everywhere else.
- **The root swipe** between Starred, Shelf and Library — a gesture she asked for and uses. Keep it (see SW-1).
- **The Journal's "today" door on the home screen.** One tap to today's entry is the phone's best shortcut; it needs to be bigger, not rethought.
- **The capture box's manners:** it never raises the keyboard at launch, it saves as she types and says so, and it files by itself when she leaves.
- **The words in the confirms** — where the thing goes, for how long, what stays. Plain and complete.
- **The Trash row's facts** (kind · where it came from · days left) and its "Open note" door for a deleted block.
- **Chapters as quiet labels** in a book's contents, and the finished ✓ on a book's name.
- **Context menus as a second road** (not the only one), and the system sort menu.
- **Book Details as a half-height sheet** with a grabber — the right native shape for a book's settings to grow into.
- **Empty states that say one true sentence** rather than drawing an illustration.
