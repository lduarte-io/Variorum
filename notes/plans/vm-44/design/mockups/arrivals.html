VM-44 mockups — Arrivals

VM-44 mockups — Arrivals. Revised after the first sitting: every frame follows plans/sitting-1-rulings.md. Today the phone has no Arrivals at all: no count on a book’s row, no door, no review, no reason and no way to add or dismiss. Notes her own tags and stars send to a book, a journal entry that has to wait, and anything an AI client proposes all pile up unseen — and the one act that touches them, Finish, dismisses them without a word.

Arrivals: “Full-screen review” · the “Open Arrivals first” button on the Finish confirm: “Keep it” · which acts show a line: “Only removals and refusals”

What was chosen, in one line. A count on the Bookcase row and on the book’s top bar; a full-screen review that says why each note waits and since when, with swipe to add or dismiss, selecting several, and a Dismiss all that asks once. The two smaller shapes (a section inside the contents, a half-height sheet) are gone.

The book is the seeded “Ideas inbox” (collects #idea; new matches wait at Arrivals). The seed has three notes waiting; two more are drawn so the frames can show a proposal with its reason and an archived note — five in all. Words are the Mac’s own (ArrivalsCopy, ArrivalsReviewCopy), in sentence case; the drafts are in design/wording-list-1.md.

19 · largest textThe review at the largest standard text size. Names wrap to two lines, the why and since lines wrap rather than truncate, and the row buttons grow. About three rows show per screen; the bulk doors keep their place at the foot.

Still open here

Undo after a dismissal. Her rule is a short line with Undo when something leaves the page. A dismissed arrival leaves the page, but the Mac has no Undo for a dismissal: the note is simply added by hand later. Drawn: the Mac’s line, without Undo. An Undo here would be new behaviour on both platforms, so it is hers to ask for.

Adding says nothing. The Mac shows a line after Add (“… added at the end of the book.”). On the phone it is an ordinary act, so no line is drawn. Say if Add should keep its line.

The values these frames are drawn with

Everything here inherits sketches/sizing-sheet.html: Study voice under the sea-glass palette; type at 13 · 15 · 17 · 20 · 24 (Iowan Old Style for her words and for names, SF for chrome); a 44 pt touch minimum; lists and prose on the paper, bars, sheets and section bands on the sea-glass room; the corrected meta ink #6A6359; the dark values are the sheet's .dk set. The shell is her rulings: the wing title band (SW-1), the Bookcase with the one-line box (SW-3), shelves as a chip row (BK-1), the bar as three words on roots and lists and never inside a note (LK-5). Frames are 402 × 874 pt, one CSS pixel to the point. Fonts fall back to Palatino or Georgia where Iowan is not installed. Dashed red outlines and the red finger mark are annotations, not design.
