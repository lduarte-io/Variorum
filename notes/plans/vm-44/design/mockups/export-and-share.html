VM-44 mockups — export, share and copy

VM-44 mockups, revised after sitting 1 — export, share and copy. Today the phone has one way out: Copy, which puts the note’s text on the clipboard. There is no share sheet anywhere in the app, no file export for a note, a book or the library, and no way to make the plain copy the Mac offers. Nothing comes in but typing.

“I think we need to word things more simply”

What she chose. Copy is the words on screen. Export asks which file: just the text, or with takes and tags (“Yes, ask which file”). A book exports as one Markdown file or a PDF in v1; a book as a folder of notes comes after v1, on the Mac and the phone (VX-1000). No message after an ordinary act, so a copy says nothing. No longer drawn: a third “Copy with takes” row, a “Copied.” line, and the folder as a v1 choice.

On a phone the share sheet is the export grammar (MobileSoul §5.7), so every door here ends in the system’s own sheet or its Files screen; the app draws only the question before it. No export writes anything to the library. Words are the Mac’s wherever the Mac has a face for the same thing (NoteExport, ProtectionCopy.exportPlaintext…, ExportModel, DocumentImportCopy); every frame holding a designer’s line wears the draft tag in its caption, and the lines are listed at the end and in design/wording-list-2.md. A finished export says nothing unless it happened in a sheet she is still looking at.

The values these frames are drawn with

Everything inherits sketches/sizing-sheet.html: Study voice under the sea-glass palette; type at 13 · 15 · 17 · 20 · 24 (Iowan Old Style for her words and for names, SF for chrome); a 44 pt touch minimum; lists and her words on the paper, bars, sheets and section bands on the sea-glass room; the corrected meta ink #6A6359 and faint #8B7F6A; the dark values are the sheet's .dk set. The shell is her rulings: the wing title band (SW-1), the Bookcase with the one-line box (SW-3), File and Open (SW-4), shelves as a chip row (BK-1), the bar as three words on roots and lists and never inside a note (LK-5), the Today button. Frames are 402 × 874 pt, one CSS pixel to the point. Fonts fall back to Palatino or Georgia where Iowan is not installed. Dashed red and blue outlines, the red finger mark and the red “open” tag are annotations, not design.
