The note — Write, end to end

VM-44, the note’s designer, revised after sitting 1. The Write surface end to end at phone-right sizes: a note at rest, what its chips open, writing with the keyboard up (the capsule, the quick row, the formatting strip and its setting), Return and backspace, the only lines the app says, the note’s menu, the blocks list with reordering, this note’s tray, the long note, a sealed note, and find. Frames read left to right; the caption under each says what she did to get there and what each control does.

What she chose, now drawn as the only answer. The bar: one right-aligned capsule, no strip; ⋯ grows into Undo · Redo · Comment · Commit, then the full menu. The formatting strip: a setting, seven big by default. Return makes a block (NT-1); backspace joins blocks and removes an empty one (NT-2). The masthead scrolls away and a slim title bar stays, with no place readout. Lines: only removals and refusals, plus Commit. Seal / Unseal; Merge with next beside previous; find now, replace later; no single-note Read mode in v1; the book strip at the foot. The side-by-side alternatives for these are removed.

Captions tagged green draw a ruling; dark green is a state (empty, loading, refusal, largest text). draft marks a frame holding a designer’s wording, listed in design/wording-list-2.md. Nothing here says how the editor works inside; these are faces and flows over the existing brains.

A — a note at rest

The prose is the screen. The title has its own row and wraps; the note’s facts are one row of chips; three tools stay in the bar. The app’s bottom bar (Starred · Bookcase · Library) never shows inside a note.
