VM-44 mockups — the Trash, Archived and tags

VM-44 mockups — the Trash, Archived and tags. Revised after the first sitting: every frame follows plans/sitting-1-rulings.md. Today the Trash puts “Put back” and “Delete Now” side by side as 14 pt-tall words 18 pt apart on every row; the Archived room is the last row under the whole Library and shows no books; the tag manager lists every wing’s tags together, twins included, and every act hides behind a long press. All three read every wing at once.

“many of the button affordances are absolutely tiny” · the three additions, all kept: “Undo after deleting a tag, Unused-tags door in the Trash, Counts in the tag list”

What was chosen, in one line each. The three rooms belong to the wing she is in and say so. The act that restores is the row’s one button; Delete now stays off the row. Kept: Undo after deleting a tag, the unused-tags door at the Trash’s foot, counts in the tag list. The bar shows on all three rooms, because they are lists. Lines: only when something leaves the page (a put-back, an unarchive, a deleted tag) and for refusals. The words are the Mac’s; the few drafts are in design/wording-list-1.md.
