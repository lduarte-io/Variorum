On keeping a commonplace book

EssaysPrivate

I started copying sentences into a notebook the winter I stopped trusting my memory for them.

The first ones were other people’s: a line from a letter, a recipe note, something overheard on the ferry. Later they were mine, and the book stopped being a record and became a place to think.

A commonplace book is not a diary. Nothing in it has to be true of the day it was written.
