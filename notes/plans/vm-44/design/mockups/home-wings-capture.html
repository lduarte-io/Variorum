VM-44 mockups — the home screen, wings and capture

VM-44 mockups — the home screen, wings and capture. Revised after the first sitting: every frame follows plans/sitting-1-rulings.md. Today the phone shows every wing at once, has no way to change wing, and its capture box is a 34 pt dashed outline whose Return key opens the note and can lose words.

“On the wing title (left)” · “Bookcase gets the screen” · “New line; File and Open buttons” · “Words on a chip” · Today: “Keep it” · “Follow the note” · “Keep the helper keys” · “Roots and lists, not notes” · “#9 seemed like a fine approach - it can be hidden behind 3 dots” · “there should be a lock icon at the top of the homescreen or in the bottom bar or something”

What was chosen, in one line each. Wings: swipe the title, tap it for the list. Wing management: in full, behind a ⋯. A note from another wing: the app follows it. Capture marks: the two helper keys. A quick note lands in the current wing’s Pool. The bar: on roots and lists, never inside a note. Lines: none for ordinary acts; a short line only when something leaves the page, for a refusal, and for a note filed or copied somewhere she cannot see. One spot is still open and drawn both ways: where the lock button sits.

On keeping a commonplace book
Essays · in Home · #essay

I started copying sentences into a notebook the winter I stopped trusting my memory for them.

It was not a diary. A diary asks what happened; a commonplace book asks what was worth keeping, which is a harder and kinder question.

The first winter I filled forty pages and reread none of them.
