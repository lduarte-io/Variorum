My picks: SW-3 (b), SW-4 (a), LK-5 (a)

SW-3 (b). The box is awkward today because it is two things at once — a thin outline that the list runs under, then a half-screen void. As a 52 pt field at the foot that grows with the words it is one thing, it is the Mac's own posture, and the Bookcase (now with shelves to show) keeps the screen. I would keep one piece of R27's intent: when she is clearly writing something long — past about six lines — offer “Open” more loudly rather than making the box taller.

SW-4 (a). It is the only option in which Return can never cost her words, and it gives the phone the multi-paragraph box she asked for. “File” and “Open” are the Mac's ⌘⏎ and ⏎ as two plain buttons. It also removes the worst moment of the lag from the common case: filing without opening never mounts the editor.

LK-5 (a). Her direction is the Mac's look; the Mac's navigation is words on a quiet chip. At 17 pt in a 56 pt bar the words stop being tiny without becoming a different app. If she wants the system's search button from (c), it fits in the masthead's round buttons instead.
