Phone sizing and spacing sheet

VM-44, grader 2. Her ruling: the look is the Mac’s (Study voice, sea-glass palette); what is wrong is that “everything is weirdly small and spaced”. So nothing here is a new design language. It is the Mac’s tokens, re-sized for a phone, as one phone layer every later mockup inherits. Sizes are in points and drawn at true size: one CSS pixel is one point at 100% browser zoom.

Reading it at physical size. A MacBook’s built-in display at its default scaling draws a point about a fifth larger than the phone does (roughly 128 points per inch against the iPhone 17 Pro’s 153). Zoom the browser to about 80–85% to see these at the size they are in the hand; at 100% they look a little bigger than they will be. The bar below is 402 pt, the phone’s width, and the square is the 44 pt touch minimum.

44 × 44 pt. As built, 103 of the 107 tappable controls are smaller than this in at least one dimension (audit G §2).

Grounds: where the sea-glass goes

One rule, taken from the Mac’s code: content panes sit on the paper (tokens.surface, white in light; the Mac’s Library, Bookcase column and Archived all use it) and chrome sits on the room (tokens.room, the sea-glass; the Mac’s bars and sheets use it). The phone today paints every screen in the room and uses the paper only for fields. With the palette on, that would put her prose on a tinted page.

How it is expressed: a phone layer over the shared tokens

The Mac reads the same VoiceTokens, so none of its six type slots (small · body · title · heading · mono · navigation) and none of its static sets change: the token pins stay byte-identical (dependency notes §1, items 5–8). The phone stops reading those slots directly and reads a role instead.

D. Contrast: the two small inks

  • The rule. Words under 17 pt need 4.5:1; glyphs, 17 pt semibold words and placeholders need 3:1. Faint never carries a sentence she has to read.
  • In effect the two inks each step down one place: the phone’s faint is about where the Mac’s muted is, and the phone’s muted is a new, darker step. Dark needs only faint lifted (2.7:1 on a card today). On a selected row’s wash the corrected muted is 4.35:1, so a selected row’s meta line takes the ink.
  • Fills and hairlines stay quiet on purpose. Paper on the room is 1.17:1 and the hairline 1.17:1 (1.36 on paper). A control is found by its size, its accent label and its shape, not by a heavier edge; field and card edges use the palette’s existing takeLine (#B7D7CB), and the Classic colours’ invisible 1.06:1 field fill stops being a problem once fields are paper on the sea-glass room.
  • The star keeps its own token (blue in every voice). The phone draws it in the accent today and never reads tokens.star (audit G §4.2).
