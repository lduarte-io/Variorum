How untitled notes and journal entries read

The rule is the one the Mac already has in core (NoteCardFace), so the phone forks nothing: a note she named leads with its name; an unnamed note leads with its own first words and previews what follows (row 1 above); a journal entry leads with its day and previews its first words (rows 3 and 6); a note with no words says “Untitled · No text yet” (row 7). Stamps use the Mac's time words — a clock time today, then “Yesterday”, a weekday, a date — never the raw day key.

book list (i)The Journal — the first words lead. The Library's unnamed-note rule applied to entries: the words are the name and the day drops to the meta line (the orchestrator's lean). Cost: the bold column no longer scans as dates, a long first sentence truncates, and it departs from the rule the Mac's core already applies to journal entries.

My pick: (b), one list face, with the day leading journal entries

She named Apple Notes, and this is its row: a bold name, then a stamp and a line of the note's own words. It answers “so i know what each note is” for every case in the seeded Library, keeps five or six notes on screen, and uses the same row anatomy as the Bookcase and a book's contents, so the four different note rows the phone has today collapse into one. No toggle: one face is calmer and is half the work.

The one dial I would leave open for her phone: if a single preview line proves thin, the same row takes a second line (four or five notes per screen) without any other change. For journal entries I would keep the day as the lead, in the Library and in the Journal alike — it is what the shared rule already does on the Mac, and a journal reads as a calendar; the preview line is what tells the days apart.
