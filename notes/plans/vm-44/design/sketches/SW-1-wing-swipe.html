SW-1 (a)The same band on the Library. 1 Under (a) the wing band is the masthead of all three roots, so the Library and its search are visibly scoped (“Search Work”); the sort door sits beside the field. The capture box rides here too, as on the Mac.

My pick: (a), the wing as the masthead

It is the only option that keeps both things she has asked for: the root swipe she already has and uses, and a quick wing swipe. It also fixes a second problem for free — today no root has a real title — by making the wing's name the title of every root, which is what tells her at a glance why a list is short. The dots make nine wings legible without a second strip of chrome above the shelf chips.

What would change my mind: if she says at the sitting that she rarely swipes between Starred, Bookcase and Library now that the bar is tappable, (c) is the better gesture — larger, in reach, and the one a new user finds without being told. (b) I would not build: it spends the most chrome where the capture box needs the room.
