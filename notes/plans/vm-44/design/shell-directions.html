The shell — three directions for navigation

Three directions for the phone's navigation — how wings, the Bookcase, the Library, quick entry and search fit together. Each is one home screen with its gestures outlined; they are assembled from the options in the four question sketches in sketches/, so choosing a direction here is the same as answering SW-1, SW-3, BK-1 and LK-5 together.

“there are no different wings (!!) so everything just shows up in one bunch but it's hard to find items in the library.”

Direction 3One home per wing. 1 No tab bar: each wing is one scroll — search, a starred strip, the shelves as folding sections, and “All notes” as the door to the Library. Wings swipe full-screen. The least chrome and the most room for words. Cost: the Library is a push away rather than a root, the starred page becomes a strip, and it is the furthest from what she uses today.

My pick: direction 1

It changes nothing she has ruled or asked for and adds the two things that are missing — a wing that is always named at the top, and shelves. Its cost is chrome, and the sketches answer that with size discipline rather than removal: every bar is a real target, and the chip row and the dots are the only additions above the list. Direction 2 is the one to take if she would rather swipe wings than roots; direction 3 is worth a look only if she finds three roots one too many once wings exist.
