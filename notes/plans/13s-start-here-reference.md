Ok, enough fun with takes. I'll tell you a bit more now about what makes the rest of the app special by telling you things that annoyed me about every other app I've tried and how I fixed them.  

Annoyance 1: Inability to read multiple notes together in other writing apps. Separate notes means separate windows / viewers. 

Fix: Variorum has books + gatherings, two different ways of seeing notes together. Think of books as a scrapbook - you put a note in there and it stays there. Gatherings are a search result - you choose your tag(s), you see all results together in book format. Both options let you see your work as a whole, read it in context with other notes, etc. Individual notes also have a Read mode, as you can see on the left. 

Annoyance 2: Lag on large notes (cough Apple notes cough) 

Fix: Variorum is powered by (xyz), resulting in a performant app that doesn't stutter when scrolling through and editing a 10k word note / 100k word book. 

Annoyance 3: Lack of good options for a real lock on my diary

Fix: Variorum is capable of entering Castle mode, an encrypted mode in which you always need a passphrase or biometrics to open it. Day One is a popular journaling app that ships an app with a lock to open and its information all available on the command line. At the same time, I didn't want opening this app to feel like starting a crypto wallet. The result is an app in which Castle mode is full privacy, full responsibility, and Standard mode is something I feel confident handing to my mom.
