3#tag across wings W-7

An all-wings search on one tag matches that tag’s path in every wing taking part — Home’s #drafts and Fieldwork’s own #drafts, two separate tags that happen to share a name — and a wing with no tag of that path (Kitchen here) simply has no group; it costs the idea that a typed tag means one tag, and the alternative, this wing’s tag only, would quietly narrow an all-wings search to one wing.
