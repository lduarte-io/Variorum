Your calls on v2

1 — does this surface succeed Block History? Your screenshots' crowding is this panel's content, re-laid: takes as rows, commits under their take, quiet folds kept, deleted takes at the surface's foot on the 30-day clock.
  • recommended — yes: ONE door ("History & lineage" — the bottom bar's history door and the block ▾ row open it), the old side panel retires, every job it had lives here.
  • alternative — keep Block History as the quick side panel AND add this; two doors to overlapping things.
2 — presentation. "At most like the way takes looks":
  • recommended — the full single-column area (as drawn), replacing the note's column while open, "‹ back" returns exactly where you were. Spacious by construction.
  • alternative — a wide side panel beside the prose (the current Block History's seat, redesigned). Narrower; compare gets cramped.
3 — the compare face.
  • recommended — side by side with changed spans washed (as drawn; a view-time comparison of two texts, nothing stored).
  • alternative — plain side by side, no washes, first pass; washes later.
4 — order inside a take's commits. The tree runs older-above. Within ONE take's commit list, today's panel runs newest-first.
  • recommended — keep newest-first inside a take (finding the latest moment is the common act; git log reads the same way).
  • alternative — oldest-first everywhere, one direction for the whole page.
