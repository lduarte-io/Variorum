Tier 1 — the glance

From any block, anywhere it shows. One line per immediate relative: the origin verb, the first words, where it lives now. Nothing opens heavy; the foot is the only door deeper.

Write · the glance popover open on the washed block

Rereading is the quiet skill nobody teaches. We praise the first pass — speed, retention, the clean sweep of a page — and say nothing of the second, where the actual reading happens.

A book read once is a rumor; read twice, it starts to become a place. The corners you missed the first time hold the furniture of the argument, and you only see them because you already know the room.

That is why the books that matter wear out their spines unevenly — the same forty pages, returned to, until the glue gives.

Tier 2 — the full surface, in Blocks

The full editor space. The open edges — your rulings

Q1 — where the glance opens from.
  • recommended — a Lineage… act in the block's context menu (and the take panel's block verbs), registry-declared, so it exists on every surface a block shows; the popover anchors on the block.
  • alternative — the fanned corner badge's popover gains a lineage section (but the badge is fan-only today: most blocks with lineage have one take, so the door would miss them).
Q2 — the tree's layout.
  • recommended — generational lanes as drawn: came-from left, the block centered, went-on-to and versions right; reads left-to-right as time, spacious by construction, and wide fans scroll vertically within their lane.
  • alternative — one indented outline (ancestors above, descendants below): denser, better for very deep chains, less of a picture.
Q3 — how much history shows per node at rest.
  • recommended — the counts line always ("3 takes · 2 commits") with history ▾ opening takes and commits in place, as on the opened node above — calm at rest, everything reachable.
  • alternative — takes + commits always expanded on every came-from node (the ruled words read literally; the surface gets tall fast).
Q4 — blocks in the Trash.
  • recommended — shown, muted, "in the Trash — restore from the Trash", no acts (as drawn): the family tree stays honest, and restore stays on its own surface as you ruled.
  • alternative — absent entirely (the tree only shows what lives; a deleted parent leaves a hole).
