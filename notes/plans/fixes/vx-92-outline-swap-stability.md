## Findings

### VX-82 — the trace (static, confirmed against this tree) and the fix design

**The current shape (post-VX-75, `BookView.swift` `OutlinePostRow` ~2388–2436):** one ZStack; the read surface is mounted iff `!(isLive && liveShowing)`; the editor iff `isLive`, at `opacity(liveShowing ? 1 : 0)`; `liveShowing` resets on the editor's `onDisappear`.

**The leave (VX-82's beat):** `liveFocusedPostID` moves (focus-elsewhere, the click-out margin's `focus(nil)`, pane defocus) → `isLive` flips false in ONE body evaluation → the editor branch unmounts immediately (its dismantle flushes pending text) AND the read branch's condition becomes true → a FRESH `ReadPostView` mounts with `contentHeight = 40` inside `StableSurface`'s reservation. The row keeps its height (the reservation) but holds NO text until `observePost`'s first async delivery → `renderFull` → `remeasure`. The read tier lays the WHOLE story out at that first render, so the blank beat is story-size-proportional — which is why she notices it on Sherlock-length stories. (Collapse is a different branch — the card renders instantly from the primed preview; not in this class.)

**The fix (the mirror, as the ticket rules):** the outgoing editor HOLDS until the incoming read surface has drawn the note at its real height.
- `ReadPostViewModel` gains the one-shot announce the inline editor already has: `onShowing`, fired from `remeasure()` the first time a height is reported with `loaded` true (the exact mirror of `InlineEditorSurface.Coordinator`'s VX-75 door); `announcedShowing` resets in `detach()` so a remade surface re-announces (idempotent at the row). `ReadPostView`/`ReadPostSurface` thread it through, default nil — every other host byte-unchanged.
- `OutlinePostRow` gains `@State readShowing` — "the CURRENTLY mounted read surface has drawn the note": set by the read surface's `onShowing`, reset by its `onDisappear`.
- The editor branch's condition becomes **`isLive || (liveShowing && !readShowing)`** (the read branch, the opacity rule and `liveShowing`'s lifecycle are untouched); hit-testing narrows to `isLive && liveShowing` so the lingering editor never swallows a click (a click during the beat falls through to the read surface's focus-request road).
- State walk, every road: forward click byte-identical (read stays while `liveShowing` false, leaves on the announce, `readShowing` resets on its exit); leave — the editor stays mounted and VISIBLE (opacity 1: `liveShowing` still true) while the fresh read surface fills underneath, and unmounts in the turn the announce lands (its `onDisappear` then resets `liveShowing`); a re-click DURING the linger finds the editor still mounted and showing — instant, better than today; far-from-viewport and collapse tear the whole ZStack down and both flags reset by `onDisappear`. No `onChange` races: every condition is row `@State` already correct at the body evaluation that acts on it.
- **Accepted consequences, said out loud for the reviewer:** (1) during the sub-second handover TWO heavy surfaces for the one note exist (the outgoing editor, hit-testing off, plus the filling read surface), beside at most the newly focused row's editor; steady state keeps exactly one live editor and P3 holds. (2) The editor's dismantle (and its pending-text flush through the per-post coordinator) is DEFERRED by the handover beat; the read surface may therefore fill one autosave behind and catch up on the flush's own delivery — transient, self-healing, the same observation road as today. (3) On the CLICK-OUT road the lingering editor can keep FIRST RESPONDER for the beat (nothing resigns it until unmount), so a keystroke landed within it still edits the note she just left — where today the instant unmount drops the keyboard to the window and the keystroke goes nowhere. Keystrokes land in the note she was just in rather than vanishing; deliberately not "fixed" with a transition-detecting resign (the `onChange` ordering class the design avoids). On the focus-elsewhere road the new editor claims the caret exactly as today.

**The revert recipe (red-on-old, run at the stage slot):** restore the PRE-fix row alone — `git show e6910fc5:App/Variorum/Variorum/Features/BookView/BookView.swift` over the worktree copy (the read tier's announce machinery stays, unused — the defaulted parameter keeps it compiling; VX-75's exact recipe) → incremental build-for-testing → `BookOutlineLandingTests` → `leavingEditModeNeverShowsAnEmptyNote` expected RED on both legs → `git checkout --` the file → suite green again. The fix is committed (`30d57020`), so the checkout is a safe restore (resiliency addendum 11a).

**The pin (red-on-old by behavioral revert):** `BookOutlineLandingTests.leavingEditModeNeverShowsAnEmptyNote` — the forward pin's mirror: story expanded → live (handover complete, read released) → `focus(nil)` (the click-out road), then every displayed turn sampled for a surface SHOWING the story (string > 1000 AND frame > 1000 pt — tightened on BOTH kinds so a filling 40-pt read surface never counts); then re-enter and leave by `focus(other)` (her clicking-around road) and sample again; the handover must COMPLETE (the read surface back, the lingering editor unmounted — never a permanent double).

### VX-91 — candidate mechanisms (to confirm or acquit by the stress probe, never by fix-first)

Her setup: everything expanded, rapid clicks across "the different options" (the navigator's note rows focus AND reveal — a landing scroll per click; prose clicks focus; the title text itself is inert). Candidates, in suspicion order:
1. **The placeholder road.** An expanded row far from the viewport renders `Color.clear` at its remembered height (`surfacePlaceholder`); `nearViewport` starts FALSE and flips on the row's own geometry report, with hysteresis (near < 2500 pt, far > 7500). A rapid-click storm = landing scrolls + focus swaps churning the column's heights; if near rows sit with `nearViewport` false (reports stale under churn, or the hysteresis mid-flip), the viewport shows the clicked row's header over `Color.clear` neighbours — EXACTLY "the title alone in the middle of the page, with no other text".
2. **The heights drop.** `reportOutlineContentWidth` fires on a >1 pt content-width change and `BookOutlineHeights.stand` drops EVERYTHING learned; `.onChange(of: outlineContentWidth)` also re-lands the top row. If anything transiently moves the measured width during swap churn, every row's height reverts to a model guess in one breath — the flash-shaped consequence + a re-land, i.e. jitter.
3. **`StableSurface` reservation refusals** (width mismatch > 1 pt; voice mismatch) collapsing a filling row to its real (tiny) height mid-storm — the column moves under the viewport, geometry reports churn, feeding 1.
4. **Landing feedback**: `outlineContentResized` re-arms the landing's quiet interval on every content-height change; a storm of height changes could hold a landing pending and re-aiming (the trace's `phase=` lines would show it).

The probe (the confirm, built FIRST): the landing harness drives rapid alternating `focus(_:caret:)` + `requestReveal` across three expanded stories while sampling per turn: the story-surface census against the rows overlapping the viewport (the "alone" oracle), neighbour top-shift oscillation (the jitter oracle), vanished anchors, and `BookOutlineGeometry.trace` for re-land loops. RED confirms a class and the diagnosis narrows from its story; GREEN after honest effort ⇒ record-only, VX-91 keeps the findings (her rule).

### VX-91 — THE DIAGNOSIS (confirmed: candidate 1, the placeholder road)

The navigator's note-row click = `focus` + `requestReveal`. The target row is far ⇒ it renders `surfacePlaceholder` (`Color.clear` at remembered height) and — the load-bearing fact — **its surfaces mount only AFTER arrival**: `nearViewport` starts false and flips on the row's own geometry report + a next-turn hop, and only then do the read surface and editor mount and START their story-sized async fills. So the arrival's viewport is headers-over-blank by construction, for (hop + mount + cold fill) long, on every far click; neighbours are far placeholders too. Rapid clicks chain these beats — the "jitter"; nothing stuck in ~20 walk minutes or the harness probe (`stormBlankTurns` was this class, mid-storm, tolerated as transient by design).

**The win (diagnosed + pinnable, so her rule permits it): pre-mount the clicked/revealed row.** The row's surface gate grows two arms — `nearViewport`, OR (`isLive` or `pendingReveal == postID`) **when a width-matched surface memory exists** — so the row she clicked mounts and starts filling AT the click and the scroll travels WHILE the text loads; her eyes land on prose. The width-matched-memory guard is load-bearing, found at the design walk: an unrecorded row's `StableSurface` reserves nothing, so an early mount would collapse a ~16k-pt placeholder to its content's birth height mid-scroll — the exact reshuffle class this run exists to calm. A row with no memory at this width keeps today's behavior (her steady-state store has persisted records for every realized story, 18d batch 4). Deliberate consequence: a LIVE row scrolled far now keeps its editor mounted (the far-release road stops occurring for the one live row) — still exactly one live editor, P3-bounded.

**The pin (red-on-old):** seed the far story's surface memory at the column's width (`rememberSurfaceSize` — the StableSurface write road, staging her steady state), then `focus` + `requestReveal` it cold; sample from the landing's settle: on the old gate the surfaces first MOUNT at arrival, so turns after settle show the story's row with no surface (RED); on the new gate the fill began at the click (GREEN).
