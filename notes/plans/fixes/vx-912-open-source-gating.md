An innocuous later edit of one membership (a pin, a reorder, a chapter move) raises its seq on the devices that have seen the edit and not on those that have not, so two devices mending the same note mid-convergence can pick DIFFERENT anchors and each demote the other's anchor: the note ends a member of nothing, both rows visible arrivals. `reading_post.created_at` is immutable and converged (the same bytes on every device holding the row), so the rank becomes a pure function of the rows; and because the key never changes, a device deciding on a SUBSET of the rows only ever demotes rows that are non-anchors of the full set too (the anchor of a superset is the subset's anchor or a row the subset lacks) — the demotions are monotone.
