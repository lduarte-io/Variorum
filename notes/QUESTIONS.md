1. **2026-07-07 · cross-app · The Fragments journal bridge — one-way, two-way, or skip? [ANSWERED — see note above]** The author's Fragments app (iOS, `~/dev/fragments`) ships a deliberately minimal "journal satellite" *explicitly designed for a co-writer journal app*: Supabase table `journal_entries` keyed `(user_id, date)`, plain-text `body`, deterministic UUIDv5 id `journal|<user>|<dayKey>`, row-LWW, RLS — the table is its only external ingress, by design (Fragments SyncSpec §8). Connecting Variorum costs the model **nothing**: the contract wants one plain-text body per day, which is exactly `renderedText` of a journal post — a one-way read-side projection push; blocks/fans/commits stay Variorum's own. Options: **(a) one-way push** Variorum→Fragments (display in Fragments' Day screen; needs a second transport + auth to Fragments' project, dayKey/UUIDv5 derivation, change detection, and an edit-war rule for days Fragments' own editor also wrote); **(b) two-way** (Fragments edits flow back via ordinary `autosave` — S5 covers conflicts — but that makes Fragments a second capture surface right as Phase 8 gives Variorum its own); **(c) skip** (accept two disjoint journals — the bifurcation is the real cost of skipping). **Decide after Phase 8** with a few weeks of real usage evidence about where she actually journals; the build is small once decided. **Status: open — author call, deliberately postponed for evidence.**
