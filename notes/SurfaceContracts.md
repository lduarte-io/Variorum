# Variorum — Surface Contracts

**What this is.** The full contract of every user-facing surface: each ruled behavior of the Library, the Bookcase and books, the note's modes and editor, search, import, the MCP door, portability and protection, stated once and plainly. It is CANON and not context-loaded: **AppSpec §12 states the stance and the cross-cutting rules; this file is read in full for every surface an effort touches, at preflight**, the same way `notes/MCPSpec.md`, `notes/CastleRules.md` and `notes/TEXTKIT2_NOTES.md` are read for theirs. A change to a surface lands here as the changed contract line, in place, plus one pointer (the plan or fix file, a ruling id, a QUESTIONS number); the dated narrative goes to `notes/AppSpecRecord.md` §12. The design records are the plan files named below and `notes/plans/phase-13-ui-design.md` (rulings R1–R94), the pre-13u round, the Act 10 correction round, the use-report rounds (`notes/plans/use-report-round-2026-09-06.md`, `-2026-09-12.md`), `notes/plans/phase-13u3-memo-gutter.md`, the 13u4 restyle, `notes/plans/phase-17-standing-fixes.md` and `notes/plans/17y-sitting-rulings-2026-10-05.md`.

## The surfaces


### The app
- **Two voices, exactly two: Terminal (leads) and Study**, each with tokens and idioms. Light is designed first. Dark is a fully black room in both voices under every palette. All fonts are macOS-shipped. Study is the fresh-store default; an existing store keeps its voice. A colour palette layers under the voice: sixteen presets plus custom hex (`theme_palette`, `theme_tint`, `theme_accent_tint`, synced), replacing only room, wash, accent, take tints and hairline through one pure rule (`PaletteDerivation`, WCAG-AA-stepped accent); `default` is each voice's shipped colours. Appearance (follow system, light, dark) is a local preference applied once as `NSApp.appearance`. No literal colour exists outside the token layer (pinned). `CalmButtonStyle` wears a token-derived disabled face app-wide. Settings' `.opening` arm says "Opening your library…".
- **The sidebar:** Bookcase · Library · Pool · Snippets · Archived, with the Trash door on the Library masthead. Library is everything. Pool is bookless notes. Snippets is the scraps area, with groups as the container noun. ⌘K is the palette; ⌘N is New Post; Settings has nine searchable panes and is tag management's home.
- **The Open list and Starred** (`notes/plans/fixes/vx-283-sidebar-library-polish.md`). The Open list grows down the sidebar to just above the sync footer, floored at 3½ rows (the sidebar's outer scroll carries the rest on a short window); Starred keeps about 7½ rows; both scroll bars sit in the list's own margin, clear of every ✕ and ★. ⇧⌘W (View ▸ Close Note) closes the shown note's row and lands on the next note in the list, else the previous, else the Library; ⌘W still closes the window; in a window merged into macOS tabs ⇧⌘W is macOS's Close Window, accepted. View ▸ Close All Notes and the list's right-click empty the list and land on the Library. A row's ✕ or right-click Close on the shown note behaves like ⇧⌘W; on any other row it removes only that row. Closing is view-only. Each list follows its current row only on the author's next act (an open, ⇧⌘W, a ✕, Close All, back or forward); a hand scroll stops the follow until the next act; a resize never pulls it back; re-selecting the shown note is not an act; each section follows on its own. An edge with more content beyond it fades: half a row deep, easing in over the row's text padding, capped at 0.8, never fully solid. An Open row drags with its title chip at the cursor; each row offers TWO half-row drops split at its midline and reaching the list's edges — the upper half lands before it, the lower half after, the two halves of one boundary resolving to one gap so the 2-pt ring line never flickers — with a short settle, an end zone under the last row reaching the last slot; the halves are live only while the list's own drag is in flight with its dragged row still drawn, and the dragged row's own slot offers nothing (VX-424). The chip lives only on the Open list's rows. A note row's context menu carries Open Beside, Open in New Window, Star/Unstar, Rename…, Move to Wing, Copy to Wing, Export Note…, Close, Close All Notes, and Delete Note… set apart — the note ▾ menu speaks the same acts, and the wing listings re-ask after any move from any door in the window (VX-425; VX-377). An untitled row reads its first words through the Library's one derivation, bounded flat in the note's length, a gap in an opening slot skipped, never thrown on (VX-580). The local, default-off setting "Hide starred notes from Open" (Settings ▸ General) keeps a starred note's row in Starred only — still open, returning on unstar or the toggle — while the ⌘⇧n badges keep the list's real numbering (VX-589).
- **Messages carry a kind on the one status seam: refusal, offer, receipt**, decided at the door. The synced `receipt_style` (toast, quiet, off; quiet by default) governs receipts only, rendered as a muted line at the bottom bar's trailing end; refusals and offers always toast. A door that did nothing speaks a refusal. An AI write's line is a receipt carrying a one-press "Open ›" door, with the session-scoped keyboard road "Show the AI's Last Change" (⌥⌘O) alive past the fade and under `receipt_style` off. A toast over the Bookcase or the Library sits above the band the shared `QuickEntryBar` marks, never on it. The Pool hosts the shared `QuietReceiptLine` at its foot for receipt-only acts from its rows (a duplicate, ⌥⌘Z, ⇧⌥⌘Z, an AI's receipt with its Open ›), composed only while a receipt shows.
- **The commit vocabulary.** The app says "commit" everywhere a savepoint was named (Block History "Commits", Settings ▸ Writing, ⌘/, receipts, the phone; both platforms, the label fence verified). The sentences that define commit say "saved moment" (QUESTIONS #206). Two ruled survivals of "savepoint" stand: one in the tour, one in the guide. **⌘S always commits:** the name field stays, and every way of leaving it but ⎋ files the commit (unnamed if nothing was typed): ⏎, clicking away, typing on (the click that returns is the leave), closing the window, ⌘S again (which files and opens no second field). ⎋ alone is don't-save. The filing commits the take the ask named, never the caret's take at leave time; one filing per press; the rename door stays ⏎-only. One shared seam (`SavepointNameField`, the leave rule, the settle-once box) serves every host (`notes/plans/fixes/vx-348-prelaunch-sweep.md`).
- **No popup greets a first open.** The app's own asks (updates, the backup folder) wait for days of use and calm moments and are always answerable in Settings (`notes/plans/phase-13r-distribution.md`). A fresh store opens on the Bookcase with quick entry at its foot and one quiet offer line in the author's approved words, with a retire-forever local flag: press it or ignore it forever (`notes/plans/phase-13s-voice-onboarding.md`). The sea-glass cards fan (#C2DDD3) is the app's one icon in every appearance, About, and the offer mark; there are no user-switchable colourways. **The tour** is a pure-rule engine with ring and cards and no dimming ever, the 22 approved frames verbatim; an off-target act re-anchors with the road-back line; Help ▸ Take the Onboarding Tutorial re-enters any time, and the guide's Help doors read "Restore Field Guide…" and "Open the Field Guide" (VX-602); the end card's readme aside asks the store whether the readme still exists before speaking; Mac-only. Every arrival at the Bookcase — a window opening on it (launch, unlock, a new window) and every later return — puts the caret in its quick entry when nothing else in that window holds the keyboard, never stealing from a field, a sheet or the tour (VX-332). The focused wing's name — "in ‹wing›", shown with one wing too — sits on both quick-entry bars and beside the window toolbar's New Note button, so a new note's landing place is never a surprise (VX-552). The capture box keeps its own undo, and a filing ends it (QUESTIONS #222).
- **The seed set:** launch = the field guide + sherlock (owned) + the empty starred notes + the disclaimer snippet. "journal" and "quicknotes" mint on demand, lowercase, as mint-time defaults only; existing stores are never renamed. Encryption Explained is gone and unrestorable; the classics have a restore door; jekyll's door is fully lowercase; the readme placeholder carries the author's Start Here text with real fans. The seeder mints and shelves the seeded content on the Welcome shelf (`ensureWelcomeShelf`, §9).
- **Capture microsyntax:** `{#tag/path}` applies the tag and `{t:"…"}` titles; only braced forms parse; tokens disappear (`Shared/CaptureMicrosyntax`, both Mac doors; the phone's face is the mobile catch-up's).
- **Keyboard family:** ⌘[ and ⌘] indent; ⌥⌘[ and ⌥⌘] go back and forward (PROVISIONAL #130); ⌘← and ⌘→ also go back and forward while no text input holds the keyboard — an interactive web view counts as one, and inside any editor they stay line start and end (VX-560); ⌥⌘N mints a new note in the focused book through that book's own foot act (VX-570); ⌥⌘S is Export Note… (VX-125); ⌘K reaches Move to Wing and Copy to Wing on the focused note, and Move to Wing on the focused book or gathering — listed only while another wing exists and never on the daily Journal — each opening a keyboard wing selector (the field focused on arrival, type to filter, ↑/↓, ⏎ chooses, esc closes) whose choice runs the right-click's act and confirm (VX-377, PROVISIONAL RB-15/RB-16; no chord — RB-6); ⌥⌘Z and ⇧⌥⌘Z are structural undo and redo; ⇧⏎ is the in-block newline; the merge chord is said on every face from the capability registry. The Shortcuts & Dictionary helper exists and shows the registry's alternate chords.

### The Library
- **The controls row** composes search, scope/mode, sort and when as one Layout (never `ViewThatFits`), the field first. It wraps onto a second line only by stable widths: the field at its prompt, never its typed text; the sort slot at its widest form; each segmented picker at its natural width. Typing, a sort pick or a search never changes the line count. At the 1240-pt default the row is one line with the info panel closed and two with it open, and the panel is never pushed off. Below the two-line widths the field collapses to a 🔍 button, a width-class decision inside the one Layout at whole points in both passes; the open field lives on its own line (open is the author's state, never the text); hidden controls sit off-row; the info panel's floor drops to 200 pt and the list gives way, never the panel; there is no window floor (`notes/plans/fixes/vx-348-prelaunch-sweep.md`).
- **List and Cards** carry a muted time stamp (`TimeStampFace`), `sort ▾` (edited, created, date, title; direction local) and the day filter. The card view is named "Cards" and always shows notes: picking Cards moves the scope to Notes, and a non-Notes scope picked inside Cards returns the view to List. Every stamp speaker hovers the whole fact through one wording brain (`TimeStampFace`) and one hover (`.stampHelp`): the Library, the info panel and note box (whose fact is the last touch), the Timeline, the search sheet, Arrivals and Archived, the pickers and the MCP pane; the raw-day-key drift copy is dead; the stamp-form setting is VX-389, deferred. The List's pinned headings in dark are drawn on the List's surface with a hairline, never a warm grey band across the black room. The Library and its search are hide-owned-exempt, with owned notes badged.
- **Search is the one finder** (`notes/plans/phase-13o-search.md`, `notes/plans/fixes/vx-83-search-followup.md`). It searches the living corpus: all live active takes, titles and worn display dates by query-time date parse, scraps, comments, names and labels; versions are fenced; archived sits behind one facet; savepoints sit behind the one explicit `+ commits` facet (the chip says "commits", lines read "commit · <day>", counts say "N commits", and a savepoint never lists without the facet); the Trash is out. It runs on a local-only derived FTS5 index with accent and case folding, the deliberate third sameness rule (the Write and Takes bars keep their pinned pair). The Library field wears the answer sheet: honest counts in the band, quiet chips, passages grouped under their notes, every "all N ›" a real road, no capped listing anywhere. ⌘K carries passages under its title hits. The `when ▾` door is one calendar popover: a mode row (on a day, between days), a voiced month grid, the armed sentence at the foot as the confirmation; nothing arms until a day is pressed; between days is two presses with the run washing in; `any day` clears. A book's masthead rests as a bare 🔍 that expands to the same engine scoped in place, gatherings included through the one membership function. `#tag` surfaces the books it flows into, top-ranked with the reason said; `#tag` alone narrows the Library to the tagged notes as its own listing rows (sort and scope live; the Blocks scope by block-level tags; archived rows marked); a plain word naming a tag offers it as a one-press chip (`tag: #name ›`). The mode facets (gathering, living, owned, finished, sealed) are facts, never tags. Wings isolate totally, with scraps the one shared room. Clicking a passage lands in Write at the block with the match selected and the find bar armed (⌘G steps on); the needle is the snippet's first contiguous matched span (whitespace and gaps of two or fewer non-newline characters coalesce, kept as written) and the landing rests in the comfort band below the shown bar. Search scales with matches, never the corpus (P1, pinned).

### The note
- **The mode row: Write · Takes · Read · Lenses · Compare · Blocks · Canvas** (⌘1–⌘7, derived from the one order). - **Takes** is single-column fan work. Its article remembers the row at the top per note for the session; an entry landing outranks the memory when the note's current block changed. The masthead `show ▾` popover filters the board by four lens forms (in X; in A, not in B; X disagrees with Y; X disagrees with the chosen takes): non-matches collapse to one-line rows, a match expands the filter-named and chosen takes, a quiet chip names the active filter, the pick is session-only per note, and a blank take is an ordinary take in every form (`notes/plans/fixes/vx-168-mcp-client-secret.md`). - **Read** carries an in-place `edit · read` switch opening on `edit`: the editable index surface with the `▸ 01` outline column, P3's one live editor through `PaneTextAuthority`. `read` is the calm page, continuous or paged; a sentence click flips to `edit` at that block through `LandingTarget`.
- **Lenses** is the editable multi-column editor and the one home of all lens machinery. **Compare** holds Outline. **Blocks** is the power-user workbench: multi-select, bulk bar, context panel. **Canvas** is a view on a post or the scraps area: spatial arrangement with the minimal one-take switcher, movement free, the switcher view-local.

Comments and callouts render in the whitespace gutter outside the text area on every surface, stacked vertically, and the prose column's measure and left edge never move for a decoration (pinned per surface); the inline-tinted comment strips are an opt-in; comment editing lives in the panel; a callout's leading glyph is one grapheme; margins are sacred; everything visible in an editing surface is editable. Write's take panel answers every routed command about a block or a take on its block, or greys the nine verbs that cut, join or add blocks, and never acts on Write's caret block beneath the sheet; ⌘⇧T closes it (QUESTIONS #221). A comment left unsubmitted in the comments panel by any close but ⎋ is kept for its block and comes back on reopen; keeping never files (QUESTIONS #223; the phone's sheet is VM-18). Write's find bar carries a position readout — "2 of 15", the Takes bar's own words — at the bottom bar's trailing end, drawn only while the bar is up and the selection sits on a match, its matches cached per needle and text generation so a step never re-walks the note (VX-441). An untitled note's title field offers its first line — or its date when the note has no words — faint in place; Tab takes the offer and ⏎ files it, exactly as the capture chip's quickfill (VX-569).

### Doors to the outside
- **Import and Copy full text** (`notes/plans/phase-13n-a-document-format.md`; the MCP door's Level 0). File ▸ Import… and the Bookcase drop read the Variorum document format: a file becomes a Pool note, a folder becomes a book; the drop is a context mint onto the solo'd shelf and lights its outline only where the drop would act; refusals name folder, file and line; receipts carry the counts and ⌥⌘Z, rendered at the main column's page foot on book surfaces (`BookNoteBox.quietReceiptHome`, PROVISIONAL). Commits and book settings do not ride the text form; the full-fidelity tier is the 13m edition.
- **The MCP door (Level 2)** is authoritative in `notes/MCPSpec.md`; read it before touching the door, its tools, grants, Settings ▸ MCP, the shared icon, the AI docs, any operation the wire calls, or Private, Seal and Own. The rules a session can break without knowingly touching MCP are kept here. The author's pending typing lands first: before a wire act that meets a take she may be typing in, the door flushes every open note's pending typing ahead of the act; a wire note delete rides her Delete door's one road (`.variorumNotesDeleted`), every pane leaving a dead note for the Library in place with focus kept. Grants are local-only and never synced (§7). A gathering is a grant place whose reach follows her tags (VX-549). A book rule an AI writes bells and never backfill-seats. The shared icon reads reach only for the rows drawn, never the corpus, and reads nothing while no client is approved (P1). `show_in_app` moves things only while Variorum is frontmost and never queues or raises windows. `list_wings` lists the wings the client holds a standing wing grant on, a granted empty wing included.
