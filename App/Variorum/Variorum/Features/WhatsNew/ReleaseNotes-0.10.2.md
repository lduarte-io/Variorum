## Your other Macs keep up when you change your passphrase

Change your passphrase or make new twelve words on one Mac, and your other Macs now notice and catch up, usually with one press. Before this fix, a Mac that had been away kept your old passphrase, and in Castle it could stop syncing with no way to catch up.

- If your passphrase changed on another Mac, Settings ▸ Protection says so and offers "Follow your account…". Type your new passphrase once and this Mac catches up.
- At the lock screen, your account's new passphrase now opens the library and catches this Mac up in the same step.
- Forgot the new passphrase? Your twelve words can catch this Mac up instead. Then set one you know with Reset forgotten passphrase.
- New twelve words made on another Mac are picked up with one press, nothing typed.
- If this Mac doesn't have your account's passphrase yet, a card above the quick entry asks you to type it once.
- Restoring a backup and syncing it again no longer says your twelve words were replaced when they weren't.
- In Castle with Touch ID, changing your passphrase asks for your fingerprint at most once.
- If you miss the Touch ID prompt at the lock screen, the button stays so you can try again.
- A few sentences are clearer: the "Sync this library" sheet's title and button, and the banner on a restored library that doesn't sync.
