## Takes got sturdier, and your fans stay where you left them

This update is all about Takes. The serious one first: pressing ⌘Z right after deleting a take could crash the app. That's gone — every take now keeps its own undo history, so ⌘Z only ever touches the take your cursor is in, and ⌥⌘Z still brings a deleted take back.

- Your open fans stay open when you hop to Write and back — the board remembers them, per note, for the session.
- Expand all and close all now show up in slot split too.
- Branch from any take: its ⌄ menu offers New branched take, and ⌘T branches the take your cursor is in.
- The bar's take word count follows the take you're typing in, keystroke by keystroke.
- Starting a selection exactly at your cursor works now — hold, then drag — in Takes and in Write.
- Takes no longer draws some of your words in a lighter face than the rest of the app.
- Takes panels say "block 2/3" instead of showing a block's id.
- A switched card on Canvas shows that take's own words, never the previous take's.
- Moving a take to the block below now says where the original went, in the words of the surface you did it on.
