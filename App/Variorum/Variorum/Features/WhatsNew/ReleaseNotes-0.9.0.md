Thank you for trying the Variorum friends beta.

## What this version is

- The first Variorum shared beyond its author. Your library lives on this Mac, encrypted on disk; sync is optional.
- Updates come to you. After a few days of writing, Variorum asks once whether to check for updates automatically. **Variorum ▸ Check for Updates…** works any time.

## Where to start

- **Help ▸ Open the Tutorial** opens the field guide: the app taught with the app itself.
- **Settings ▸ Data Safety** shows your backups and lets you keep a second copy in a folder of your choosing, iCloud Drive among them.
