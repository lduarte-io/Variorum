## What this version is

- The first ever version of Variorum shared beyond my own testing. Your library lives on the Mac you download, encrypted on disk. Sync is optional and free.
- Updates come to you — after a few days of writing, you’ll be asked once if you want to check for updates automatically. **Variorum ▸ Check for Updates…** works any time under the menu title. I’ll let you know when you should look for an update — there will be a couple in the upcoming days.

## Where to start

- **Help ▸ Open the Tutorial** opens the field guide, which is the app teaching you how to use it. I’ll be adding an onboarding flow and rewriting the tutorial in upcoming days to be in my own words, but feel free to let me know any section that confuses you.
- **Settings ▸ Data Safety** shows your backups and lets you keep a second copy in a folder of your choosing. iCloud Drive is an option.
