**Your library, without the doubles.** Signing a fresh install into your account no longer
duplicates the starter content — the field guide, the classics gathering, starred notes,
Quicknotes, the first quick note and the tutorial scraps each fold to one copy when both are
untouched. Anything you've touched is always kept.

**The field guide is there from the start.** A new library is born with its guide on the
Welcome shelf — no restore needed. The guide was also read end-to-end against this release
and corrected where the app had moved on. Already running an earlier build? Your copy stays
as it is until you press **Help ▸ Restore Tutorial…** — one press fetches the updated
edition.

**Jekyll steps back.** *The Strange Case of Dr Jekyll and Mr Hyde* no longer seeds into new
libraries — add it any time from Help ▸ Restore. Existing libraries keep what they have.

**Drags work now.** Dragging books onto shelves, dragging chapters in the Book Panel and
reordering the Open list were all silently dead — the app had never declared its own drag
types. All fixed. (Reordering books by hand steps back for one release and returns, done
properly, in a small update.) And what you drag is now a neat title chip riding under your
cursor, not a scattered line of text.

**A starred shelf.** The Bookcase strip opens with "starred" — your starred books in one
place. Drop a book on it to star it.

**Arrivals, where you can see them.** A book's waiting arrivals live on a small tray icon
beside its settings gear — calm when nothing waits, present when something does. Click a
waiting note to read it before deciding.

**Pickers that search.** The rule editor's tag pickers and the add-to-book picker filter as
you type — and can create the tag or book right there.

**The app follows you across wings.** Opening a note from another wing switches the app to
that wing, with a quiet "Now in …" note.

**The Library scrolls everything.** The 40-note window is gone — List and Cards now scroll
your whole collection, in every sort, reading a little at a time as you go. And under All,
a big bookcase keeps to its own half so your notes always show.

**The note's index keeps up.** Scrolling the index beside a long note used to stutter and
leap — a quick flick could throw you to the top. It now moves at the display's own pace,
the whole way down.

**Smaller things.** The № census and the order menu are now quiet twins under their own
marks — nothing covers anything · the Bookcase strip's shelves sit on soft squares, the one
you're in filled · picking Cards shows notes (and picking a books view steps you back to
List — no more empty "All") · Terminal's cards got room to breathe · the Library's card
view is named "Cards" · the chapterless group reads "unfiled" everywhere · a finished
book's drag refusals tell you the way back ("reopen it first") · one cursor in the
quick-note box · clicking a date-named note's title no longer shifts the date · Canvas
became a debug-only surface.
