# Variorum 0.10.7

## The tour, rebuilt around the prince

- The onboarding tour now walks the prince, the bilingual book every new library starts with, and it has grown: thirteen steps and a short keep-going leg.
- New stops along the way: choose the Italian take of a paragraph and bring the English back, visit Read and its edit · read switch, open a saved lens with the whole chapter in Italian beside the English, meet Compare and Blocks, climb into the book's own views — Outline, Index, Book and Read — and come back down to where you write.
- The tour always finds its footing: each step brings its target onto the page, a note that opens in the wrong mode is switched to the one the step teaches in, and your own choices are never overridden.
- If the prince is missing when the tour starts — or chapters of it have been deleted — it is restored through its own door first, and only what deletions broke is brought back. Your edits to the book survive.
- The keep-going leg ends in Settings, with the look of the app and the MCP pane in view.
- The welcome offer is now a card above the quick entry: yes starts the tour, no puts it away for good, and Help ▸ Take the Onboarding Tutorial starts it any time.

## Sea glass, the new default

- A new colour palette drawn from the app's own icon: sea glass. It is now the default, so a library that never picked a palette wakes up wearing it.
- The colours an unset library wore before live on as Classic: Settings ▸ General ▸ Colour palette ▸ Classic brings the old look back with one click.
- Seventeen palettes now, plus your own hex.

## A letter, and the classics

- A welcome letter from Lillian now arrives in every new library as the note "readme", starred and at the top of your Library.
- Help ▸ Restore “readme” brings the welcome note back whenever you want it.
- Each sample book now has its own restore door in Help — "the adventures of sherlock holmes" included — replacing the old Restore the Sample Library item.
- The field guide's first chapter now begins with the move from a book into a note, and covers your library's keys and safety up front.
