**A new face.** Variorum's icon is sea-glass now.

**Comments read better beside your writing.** Side comments stack as cards next to their paragraph; a card grows
to the paragraph's height and scrolls inside when the note is long. A deleted comment can now be deleted for good,
and the comments panel places its caret, types and undoes the way a text editor should.

**More for AI clients.** For anyone using Variorum's MCP door:
- With Variorum closed, a connecting client now hears "Variorum must be open" instead of "connection closed".
- Running a client's setup again simply replaces the old setup and keeps everything you shared — no need to deny it
  first.
- At the edit level, an AI can now do nearly everything you can at the keyboard on what you shared: delete, archive,
  label and lock takes; restore and rename commits; rename notes; rename, finish, archive and delete books and
  chapters; reorder notes and chapters; take notes out of books; delete and archive notes; and edit, delete or
  restore comments.
- At the lower levels it can add many takes or comments in one go, delete comments it left itself, link two notes as
  versions so Compare pairs them, and remove tags.
- Every change is still one ⌥⌘Z, named after the client, and undoing never overwrites a change you made since.
  Private, Seal and Own stay yours alone.
- Gatherings show the shared-with-AI icon when a wing share reaches them.

**Castle, steadier.** Choosing Castle while signed out of sync now works, for this Mac. Reopening right after the
switch is safe. The offer to delete old backups also finds the library copies Variorum kept aside, finishing Castle
reminds you to delete Markdown exports made before it, and entering Castle from Settings ▸ Protection keeps you on
Protection. Opening an older copy of your library now says first when that copy isn't in Castle. The field guide's
privacy lesson no longer overstates what's encrypted (Help ▸ Restore Tutorial… brings the corrected guide to an
existing library).

**Settings and small things.** Settings has a rewritten General pane, and a wider window whose title no longer runs
over the pane. The app menu drops the system's Services clutter. Tags have a clear delete button, with a
confirmation. A gathering's foot has a New note button, like a book's. Small menu text reads at a comfortable size.
Locking with Settings open no longer leaves a blank window, Choose works on a take you just made in Write, and
stepping between takes (⌃⌘→) or across lens columns carries your editing along.
