**More your AI can do.** For anyone using Variorum's MCP door:
- An AI can add paragraphs to a note: at the end with Write new, anywhere with Edit.
- An AI can duplicate a note the way Duplicate as Version does, so Compare lines its rewrite up with your original row by row, and it can open Compare to show you two notes side by side when you ask.
- An AI can save a lens by take label instead of naming every take, and its own takes are labeled with just its name, like "claude-code · english".
- Writes answer with the ids of everything they made and a fingerprint of the words stored, so an AI can check its work without reading the whole note back.
- Long notes are read in pages small enough for an AI to take in whole, and an AI can look up its own recent acts to see whether a call went through after a dropped connection.
- Clearer answers: notes made in a book that tags its notes say so, tagging a note twice is simply fine, and every refusal the door knows of is in plain words.
- A connected AI is pointed to a guide page on stargazing.tech that explains the door, and the field guide's chapter on AI is refreshed. To get the new chapter, choose Help ▸ Restore Tutorial… (your old guide goes to the Trash for 30 days).
- An AI can no longer learn the name of a book it can't read by importing one with the same name.
- In the book panel, a chapter that is one note wears that note's shared-with-AI mark.
- The undo warning no longer shouts "BEFORE".

**The sidebar and the Library, tidier.**
- The Open list grows down the sidebar to just above the sync row, and its scroll bar no longer covers a row's ✕ (or a Starred row's ★).
- ⇧⌘W closes the note you're looking at and moves you to the next one in the Open list, so you can close them all quickly; View ▸ Close All Notes clears the list. Clicking ✕ on the note you're looking at does the same as ⇧⌘W.
- The Open and Starred lists fade softly at an edge where there's more to scroll, instead of cutting a row in half. The Open list follows the note you open, close or go back to, and never pulls back from where you scrolled.
- Dragging a note in the Open list shows a line where it will land, carries its title under the pointer, and settles into place.
- Messages no longer cover quick entry on the Bookcase.
- The Library's search, scope, view, sort and day controls wrap onto two lines when the window is narrow, keeping the info panel on screen, and typing, searching or changing the sort never makes them jump.
- In dark mode, the Library List's "books" and "notes" headings no longer draw a grey band across the black room.
- The View menu no longer shows two separators in a row.
