# Variorum 1.3

This update brings bigger changes to how you see and interact with version control! The history area of blocks got a major update that makes it easier to see and work with both commits and block lineage. There are also some small quality-of-life changes: you can pin notes within books for easier access, private notes have an icon now, and more details are in the list below.

- Every block now carries its whole story in one place: click History to read any saved moment as a full page, with what changed against now shown as gentle washes, every take alongside, and ↑/↓ flipping between moments. Compare any two moments — "now" is always offered as a third. And the new Lineage view shows where a block came from — what it was divided from, merged with, or extracted out of — with Put back to restore an original and Bring back to lift any relative's text in as a fresh take.
- A book's waiting notes became a real review: open Arrivals and every row says why it waits and since when. Select many at once, add or dismiss them together, or judge from the keyboard. Opening a waiting note never files it.
- Pin a note to the top of its book: pinned notes float above the book's order — within their chapter in a chaptered book — with a quiet mark on the table of contents.
- Search reaches deeper: the Blocks facet lists every block-level match — takes, take labels, block names, block comments and snippets, with saved moments behind + commits — and the counts follow whichever facet you pick. Results can order by any of the Library's sorts, with best match leading. A quiet search field now sits at the top of the sidebar, and the month grid answers the arrow keys.
- Private is visible now: while an AI client is approved, a Private note or book wears a small closed eye wherever it's listed. Hover it to see why it's private; click it to change your mind.
- Books settled into their doors: a book's menu is the same in every list that shows it, Book Settings gained Move to Wing, two wings may hold books with the same name, and the book's header reads as one line.
- A book and its notes live in one wing, always — if two syncing devices ever disagree about that, the displaced notes wait at the book's door instead of vanishing.

## For connected AI tools

- A tool with read access may now propose notes to a book, with its reason. A proposal never enters the book — it waits as an arrival wearing the tool's name and its why, for you to judge.
- New reads: a note's lineage (one hop of its family), listings that filter by tag and carry tags and dates, a note's tags in its answer, and an honest one-sentence reply for an empty note.
- Anything beyond what you've shared is simply not listed — lineage included.
- The sidebar's AI trail grew controls: dismiss a line, drag it out, clear a tool's lines at once, and a setting for how the trail shows.
