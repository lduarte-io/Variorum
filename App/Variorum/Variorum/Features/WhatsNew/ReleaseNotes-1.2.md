# Variorum 1.2

- The Library remembers where you were: coming back from a note lands you at your place, with the note you left quietly marked.
- The daily Journal is yours to move — or delete: carry it to another wing and ⌘J follows it; delete it and the next day's entry starts a fresh one. New Entry starts a fresh entry every time, so a day can hold several, and ⌥⌘J dates the note you're in a day earlier — for the entry written after midnight.
- Wings from the keyboard: ⌘1–⌘9 jump to your wings while you're not in a note, picking a wing lands you in its Bookcase, and a long wing name scrolls in the space it has.
- Spellcheck has a home again: Edit ▸ Spelling and Grammar is back in the menu, and ⌘; runs a one-time check without turning checking on.
- Pressing Return inside a word carries the whole word down even beside punctuation, where it used to refuse.
- Two cleanup doors: the Trash can delete every empty block with no history in one sweep, and Settings ▸ Tags — which now names and follows your wing — can delete every unused tag.
- Smaller fixes: the New Book dialog has its Cancel button back, and the Trash's messages no longer cover its last row.

## For connected AI tools

- Books can move between wings, a book's notes can be reordered, notes can be born tagged, and a tag can be replaced everywhere it's worn.
- A tag an AI applies never files a note into a private book or your daily Journal — it waits at the door as an arrival, for you to decide.
- Locking your library keeps AI connections alive but tells them nothing: while locked, tools hear only that the library is locked, and everything resumes when you unlock.
- The sidebar's AI section keeps a trail of what each tool showed, opened and edited.
