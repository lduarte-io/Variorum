**A new icon.** Variorum now wears the fan of cards — one mark, the same in light and
dark, in the Dock and the Finder, all of the time.

**Search, everywhere.** The Library's field is now a real finder over everything living —
your notes' full text, titles, takes, comments, scraps, block names — with honest counts,
passages grouped under their notes, and every "all N ›" a real road. Type `#tag` to narrow
the Library to the tagged notes; type a word that names a tag and the tag is offered as a
one-press chip; a **+ commits** facet brings your saved commits into the results; the
**when ▾** door is a small calendar — a day, or between days, in two presses. Clicking any
passage lands in Write with the match selected and the find bar armed (⌘G steps onward).
A book's masthead carries the same search, scoped in place.

**Your library travels.** **File ▸ Send Library to Another Mac…** writes a sealed copy and
its key file side by side — AirDrop the folder whole. **File ▸ Open Library…** adopts one:
your current library steps aside in full before anything lands, and the adopted library
holds quietly — nothing syncs until you press **Resume Sync**. Settings ▸ Data Safety gains
**Restore from Backup**, each backup saying which credential opens it.

**AI can visit — strictly on your terms.** Settings ▸ MCP runs a local server while
Variorum is open, so MCP clients — Claude Code, Codex, or any other — can read and write
here through Variorum's own operations. Nothing is readable until you grant it: grants are
per client and per place (a note, a book, a wing), at four levels from read to edit, each
one revocable, every act listed in plain words. A client's edits arrive with a commit
before and after, and its new text lands as takes beside your own. Your Journal, and
anything you mark Private, only ever answer a one-time ask. Each client connects with its
own setup key, so nothing can borrow a name. The field guide grew a chapter on all of it —
and if none of this appeals, leave the switch off and nothing listens.

**Import, and the full text out.** **File ▸ Import…** (or a drop on the Bookcase) reads
Variorum's own export format back — a file becomes a note, a folder becomes a book with
chapters, takes and tags riding along; any export re-imports whole. **Copy full text** puts
a whole note on the clipboard in the same format, takes included.

**Takes, filtered by a lens.** In Takes, a **show ▾** door filters the board by your saved
lenses — what's in one, what's in one but not another, where two disagree, where a lens
disagrees with the chosen takes. Non-matches fold to a line; the filter forgets itself when
the note closes.

**The tour.** A brand-new library now opens calmly — no popup, just a quiet offer line —
and a guided tour walks the first steps. On any library, **Help ▸ Take the Tour** enters it
whenever you like. The app also now says **commit** everywhere a saved moment is named —
one word for one idea.

**Under the hood.** A week of steady fixes across sync, key custody and the editor — plus
the usual pass of smaller refinements throughout.
