# Variorum 1.4

This update hardens the places where your writing meets the outside world — syncing while you type, unlocking offline, and an AI's edit landing in a note you have open. The heart of it: nothing you write can be silently overwritten anymore, and nothing that arrives can be lost to your typing — whichever lands second, both texts survive. Around that core there's a wide round of polish: Compare, callouts, quick notes, History's wording, the guide, and a calmer, sturdier footing for connected AI tools.

- A sync change arriving in the middle of your typing can no longer overwrite your words — or lose its own. One text keeps the take and the other becomes a take labelled "conflict" on the same block, side by side in the fan, ready to compare, merge or discard.
- When an AI, another device or another window rewrites the very take you're editing, the handover is clean everywhere: the pane steps back to reading and shows the new words, and a keystroke landing in that instant is kept as its own take — never written over the incoming text.
- Unlocking with your passphrase while offline no longer leaves sync locked for the whole session. The unlock finishes by itself the moment the server can be reached, and the network coming back quietly runs a sync round on its own — on the Mac and the phone alike.
- Restoring a saved moment now keeps one quiet automatic moment of the words it replaces, so a restore can never cost you text you had just typed.
- Callout text lines up exactly with plain text now — the coloured frame bleeds outward instead of squeezing the words.
- Compare breathes: columns keep their full text measure, comment cards appear only when a column is wide enough (a slim badge lane otherwise), and you can start a block's first comment thread right there with Comments… or ⌘⇧'.
- History reads honestly on lineage: a take that lives on as lineage shows "against as kept" and counts "changes to the take as kept", and the Lineage view names Home the way the wing switcher does.
- The quick-note pad can aim a capture at another wing — a quiet wing word in its header once you have a second wing — and pressing return twice quickly no longer files the same note twice.
- ⌘⏎ files a new note without opening it, from both entry bands, and the guide teaches it.
- Search's header now follows the facet you click, and Make a Gathering from Settings ▸ Tags lands in the wing you're looking at, reading that wing's own tags.
- The badge switches split in two: "Show ownership badges" (back in Settings ▸ Editor) governs the book marks, while a new switch at the foot of Settings ▸ MCP shows or hides the shared-with-AI and Private marks.
- Settings ▸ Acknowledgments now credits every library the app ships with, each license in full, on the Mac and the phone.
- The guide's protection chapter gained the compromise-recovery recipe — the three moves in order, what each one protects, and the honest limit of what recovery can promise.
- Under the hood of sync: books that claim their notes now settle ownership identically on every device, an outdated spare key file left beside your library by an earlier version is tidied away at open, and the What's New sheet no longer breaks long bullets apart.

## For connected AI tools

- A tool whose change lands is always told the truth, even when the activity log couldn't record the change — that failure comes to you as a single notice instead of a false error to the tool.
- A stalled tool can no longer slow the app down: every connection writes on its own lane, and switching the server off never waits on a stuck client.
- The server's start button no longer races itself: a cancelled start can't clear a healthy server's state or claim that another Variorum is already serving.
- Tools can now remove a version link they made, and one undo puts it back.
- The MCP pane came back together as one place: the trail dial beside the show dial, client groups that fold, headings that follow each setup's label, and the badges row at the pane's foot.
