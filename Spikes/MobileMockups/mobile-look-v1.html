What the rain kept

  5. The keyboard bar: the eight acts shown (B · I · # · lists · ☑ · ⇥ · takes · ⋯) — right set, right order?
  6. The takes sheet: sheet-over-page (page visible behind) vs. full screen? Card density?
  7. The Read page: the chapter caption + centered page meta — enough chrome, or still too much?
