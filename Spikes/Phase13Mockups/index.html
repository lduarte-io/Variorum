Round 1 — three directions

Variorum · Phase 13 design sittings · 2026-07-17 · throwaway explorations, built from sitting 1's anchors

Direction A

The Study

The Phase 11 mockup language, matured. Warm sepia room, white paper posts, serif prose, dotted seams between blocks, tinted take cards, a bookshelf home. The anchor you named — pushed further.

 Direction B

The Terminal Study

Mono text, generous spacing, almost zero chrome. The fastest possible path from opening the app to typing. "No other things going on" as the whole design. The shelf is a quiet list, not covers.

 Direction C

The Reading Room

iBooks as an editing surface. Page-like reading flow, chrome that disappears at rest, finished books showing nothing but the text — and the takes machinery summoned per-block when you reach for it.
How to read these: each page shows the same three moments — the shelf (home), a book open, and the editor with blocks + takes — plus a dark-grey sample (your Gemini-grey note). They are deliberately divergent; per your own framing, the likely outcome is one direction leading as the default and the others informing what the theme system must be able to span. React to anything at any level: a whole direction, one color, one detail. Nothing here is committed.
