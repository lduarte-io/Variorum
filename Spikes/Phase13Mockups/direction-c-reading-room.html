II · THE LONG MIDDLE

Progress in the middle of anything is invisible from inside it. The days repeated with small variations, the way a theme repeats until suddenly it is a fugue.

We measured the crossing not in miles but in habits formed and abandoned. Coffee at dawn lasted eleven days. The log-keeping lasted longer, because it lied less.

The storm found us on the fourteenth day, and I have three ways of telling it.

· · ·

Afterward the boat was smaller and we were larger, and neither of us mentioned it.
